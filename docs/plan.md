# The engine's plan · `plan:nrz:engine-plan`

## Order and dependencies · `sec:nrz:plan-order-and-dependencies`

The execution order is documentation generator, exemplar register, numerical feasibility, units design, units and numerical foundations, certified matrix core, initial physics core, advanced matrix domains, and accuracy and benchmarks. The exemplar-register bite is numbered 1b between bites 1 and 2. This order realises the architectural sequencing and mapping while supplying reference targets before numerical experiments (`dec:vision:sequencing-adjustment`), (`tab:vision:bite-mapping`), (`dec:exemplars:exemplar-bite`).

Documentation generation supports every subsequent bite. The exemplar seeds and adversarial fixtures precede feasibility; feasibility constrains the units decision; that decision and validated scalar contracts precede the foundations; the foundations support the certified matrix core. Physics uses only supported matrix capabilities. Advanced domains require their own certificates before dependent physics capabilities expand. Consolidation evaluates the capabilities established by the preceding bites.

Every numerical bite carries its own accuracy tests and representative measurements. The final stage broadens that evidence. Each bite declares its supported domain, assurance, failure behavior, and exclusions; failure to establish a required guarantee either narrows the explicitly supported deliverable or leaves the bite incomplete (`rule:vision:verification-is-continuous`), (`rule:vision:completion-boundaries`). A cited exemplar contributes its stated take and refusal (`rule:exemplars:take-and-refuse`).

## The bites · `sec:nrz:plan-bites`

**Step (Bite 1: Documentation generator)** · `step:nrz:bite-documentation-generator`

Deliverable: `packages/docgen`, crate `nrz-docgen`, ingests envelope-carrying TOML, derives deterministic JSON datasets, uses a document manifest, and renders minijinja templates beside their Markdown outputs. Its five renders are the architecture, exemplar register, plan, readme, and contributing guide. It supplies `nrz-docgen data`, `nrz-docgen render`, and `nrz-docgen check`; `check` exits 3 on drift. The first usable commit includes the generator's own documentation sources and renders and establishes the same generation rule for all five documents. Exact envelope fields and owner integration are resolved against the declared linter snapshot.

Verification: regeneration in an isolated output area matches committed bytes and output inventory, and another generation produces no change. Drift, missing outputs, and stale outputs are detected. The generator compiles under the declared lint posture, and labels, ownership, and license classifications pass the linter. This realises (`prin:vision:documentation-is-reproducible`), (`tab:vision:package-structure`), (`dec:vision:documentation-layout`), (`rule:vision:documentation-envelope-and-derivation`), (`rule:vision:fixed-point-gate-and-bootstrap`), (`dec:vision:labels-and-ownership`), (`rule:vision:metadata-and-licenses`), (`rule:vision:gate-of-record`), and (`cav:vision:estate-schema-and-reproducibility`). It uses (`ex:exemplars:estate-tools-and-templated-documentation`). It leaves out numerical engine implementation, problem-tree seeding, and invented envelope or owner schemas.

**Step (Bite 1b: Exemplar register)** · `step:nrz:bite-exemplar-register`

Deliverable: maintain the register's text as one of the generator's five renders, seed `problems/` with the seven reference problems in the prescribed entry shape, and bind the adversarial fixtures to the numerical-feasibility tests. Each problem identifies its physical model, inputs with units and declared uncertainty, independently justified expected observables, bounded or estimated or excluded error classes, and the architectural claim it stresses (`conv:exemplars:problem-entry-shape`). This bite follows the generator and precedes feasibility (`dec:exemplars:exemplar-bite`), (`rule:exemplars:adversarial-fixtures-gate-first`).

Verification: all seven seeds satisfy the entry shape, their expected observables have a stated derivation or independent source, their citations resolve, and their documentation passes the fixed-point check. The adversarial test bindings establish that certification meets those fixtures before physics problems. This realises (`rule:vision:verification-locations`), (`rule:vision:independent-numerical-evidence`), and (`dec:vision:documentation-layout`). It uses the documentation pipeline (`ex:exemplars:estate-tools-and-templated-documentation`); the seven seeds are (`prob:exemplars:spring-mass-truss`), (`prob:exemplars:resistor-network`), (`prob:exemplars:coupled-oscillators-normal-modes`), (`prob:exemplars:kepler-two-body-orbit`), (`prob:exemplars:damped-driven-harmonic-oscillator`), (`prob:exemplars:heat-conduction-one-dimensional`), and (`prob:exemplars:pendulum-gravity-estimate`). Its adversarial targets are (`ex:exemplars:hilbert-matrices`), (`ex:exemplars:kahan-and-wilkinson-matrices`), and (`cex:exemplars:rump-polynomial`). It leaves out solver implementations and any claim that seeding a problem establishes engine support for it.

**Step (Bite 2: Numerical feasibility)** · `step:nrz:bite-numerical-feasibility`

Deliverable: record the safe integration capabilities of a selected faer version, prototype the `f32` and `f64` scalar and conversion contracts, exercise representative candidate operations, and validate a minimal independent residual and norm path with the baseline approximate-inverse certificate. Record arithmetic assumptions, supported environments, dependency trust boundaries, certificate costs, and limitations before broad public APIs are fixed. This realises (`dec:vision:precision-selection`), (`rule:vision:precision-conversions`), (`dec:vision:independent-verification-arithmetic`), (`def:vision:certified-solve-contract`), (`dec:vision:inverse-norm-certificate`), (`rule:vision:backend-boundaries`), (`cav:vision:backend-capability-uncertainty`), (`cav:vision:validated-arithmetic-assumptions`), (`cav:vision:transitive-unsafe-code`), and (`cav:vision:lint-feasibility`).

Verification: exact small cases and independent enclosures establish the primitive arithmetic behavior, including cancellation, subnormals, overflow, fused operations, and non-finite cases. Candidate verification uses no assumed backend reduction order. Adversarial fixtures precede physical examples and demonstrate conservative enclosures or explicit refusal without false success. Representative prototypes meet the actual lint configuration. The certificate and error-analysis references are (`ex:exemplars:intlab`) and (`ex:exemplars:higham-accuracy-and-stability`); arithmetic references are (`ex:exemplars:error-free-transformations`), (`ex:exemplars:fptaylor-and-gappa`), and (`ex:exemplars:interval-arithmetic-jl`); the backend boundary is tested against (`ex:exemplars:faer`). Adversarial targets are (`ex:exemplars:hilbert-matrices`), (`ex:exemplars:kahan-and-wilkinson-matrices`), and (`cex:exemplars:rump-polynomial`). This bite leaves out a finalized units representation, production physics, automatic extended-precision admission, and unsupported backend guarantees.

**Step (Bite 3: Units design)** · `step:nrz:bite-units-design`

Deliverable: choose the dimension representation through equivalent compile-only prototypes, then render the decision and rejected alternatives. The decision covers seven SI exponents, normalization and equality, inference, semantic kinds, canonical conversions, affine coordinates, supported powers and roots, homogeneous matrix owners, heterogeneous blocks, solve dimensions, and factor dimensions. This realises (`dec:vision:homogeneous-numerical-leaves`), (`rule:vision:heterogeneous-assembly`), (`dec:vision:storage-and-scalar-wrapping`), (`tab:vision:dimension-representation-candidates`), (`req:vision:units-design-criteria`), (`rule:vision:canonical-quantities-and-affine-coordinates`), (`req:vision:powers-and-factor-dimensions`), and (`open:vision:dimension-encoding-and-semantic-kinds`).

Verification: compile-success and compile-failure prototypes demonstrate dimensional equality, equivalent-expression normalization, multiplication, division, integer powers, supported roots, matrix solves, and useful rejection messages. Compare soundness, expressiveness, inference, diagnostics, compilation cost, and lint compatibility in that order. Quantity and affine semantics use (`ex:exemplars:mp-units`); inference and implementation comparisons use (`ex:exemplars:fsharp-units-of-measure`), (`ex:exemplars:uom-and-dimensioned`), and (`ex:exemplars:nalgebra`). Physical dimension cases use (`prob:exemplars:spring-mass-truss`), (`prob:exemplars:resistor-network`), (`prob:exemplars:coupled-oscillators-normal-modes`), and (`prob:exemplars:heat-conduction-one-dimensional`). This bite leaves out production numerical kernels, arbitrary per-element dimensions, and implicit support for roots or semantic distinctions the chosen representation cannot express.

## The import procedure · `sec:nrz:plan-import-procedure`

**Procedure (Import commit series)** · `alg:nrz:import-commit-series`

The lane ships named commits after folding its squash working commits. The superloop fetches the ref into quarantine in the private bare and reviews the complete diff as text. It pushes the candidate to the straka hub and runs the gate of record over that candidate (`gate:nrz:verification-of-candidate`). After acceptance, it cherry-picks the named commits onto main one by one, each signed. A server-generated lock file is amended into the commit that changed dependencies, with the resulting commit signed. The superloop runs the host linter's `check` and `fmt --check` over the tip, then pushes main to the private bare, the hub, and the forge. The imported series preserves independently reviewable, green units and keeps generated artifacts with their causes (`rule:vision:meaningful-commit-series`).

## The gate of record · `sec:nrz:plan-gate-of-record`

**Gate (Verification of the candidate)** · `gate:nrz:verification-of-candidate`

The gate fetches the candidate and checks commit policy over every commit past the base: a Conventional Commits subject of at most 72 characters, a non-empty body explaining why, and no squash prefix. The full meaningful-unit policy also governs review and acceptance (`rule:vision:meaningful-commit-series`), (`rule:nrz:coherent-main-commits`), (`rule:nrz:commit-subject-and-body`), (`rule:nrz:generated-artifacts-with-causes`), (`rule:nrz:fold-working-squashes`).

The gate runs the following checks:

- The pinned nightly rustfmt check.
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`.
- `cargo doc --no-deps` with documentation warnings denied.
- `cargo test --workspace --no-fail-fast`.
- `nrz-docgen check`.
- The host linter's `check` and `fmt --check`.

The verification evidence includes appropriate package and integration tests, dimensional compile-success and compile-failure cases, architecture dependency checks, and relevant supported feature configurations (`rule:vision:gate-of-record`), (`rule:vision:verification-locations`), (`rule:vision:layer-enforcement`). Workspace rustc and clippy groups are denied except nursery and cargo, which are configured to warn; the gate's warnings-denied clippy invocation remains as stated. Unsafe code is forbidden. Missing documentation, missing Debug implementations, unreachable public items, unwrap, todo, unimplemented, dbg, and print macros are denied. Fallible I/O serves routine tooling output. The documentation check verifies committed bytes, inventory, and the generation fixed point (`rule:vision:fixed-point-gate-and-bootstrap`).
