## Physical model and independent answers · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-model`

**Definition (Series one-port and autonomous state)** · `def:nrz:lc-resonance-model`

Take ideal positive `L` and `C` in series, with passive signs and the phasor convention `Re(V_hat exp(i*omega*t/radian))`. Define `a=omega/radian` as a derivative rate, so `Z=i*X`, `X=a*L-1/(a*C)` and `Y=1/Z` where `Z` is nonzero. For source-free motion let current charge the capacitor: `C*dv/dt=i`, `L*di/dt=-v`. The state `x=(v,i)` has operator `A=[[0,1/C],[-1/L,0]]` and characteristic equation `s^2+1/(L*C)=0`, where `s` is a Complex rate. A positive angular frequency is `omega0=radian/sqrt(L*C)`; `f0=omega0/(2*pi rad)` and `T=2*pi rad/omega0`. The usual coordinate shorthand is `omega=2*pi*f`; its physical meaning includes the full-cycle angle.

The reference uses `L=25 mH=1/40 H`, `C=10 microfarad=1/100000 F` and the separate autonomous initial state `v=5 V`, `i=0 A`. The impedance samples use `omega=1000,2000,4000 rad/s`. The driven frequency-domain relation and the source-free initial-value energy check are different experiments on the same ideal elements. Absence of declared input uncertainty is not proof that a manufactured element is exact (`prin:vision:guarantees-name-scope`).

## Units, kinds and pi · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-units`

**Rule (Three rate meanings and an angle difference)** · `rule:nrz:lc-resonance-kinds`

Angular frequency in rad/s and cyclic frequency in Hz both have time exponent minus one, but retain distinct named kinds. A state eigenvalue is a growth/rotation rate in `s^(-1)`, with a third rate kind; its imaginary magnitude becomes angular frequency only through the explicit radian factor. Neither descriptor equality nor ordinary same-kind measure conversion implements `omega=2*pi*f`. The relation must retain its full-cycle angle, source declarations and original operations. The corpus uses one-second and one-hertz exact references to state `f0=(1 s/T)*(1 Hz)` without an implicit kind cast. The phase is the angle difference `arg(V_hat)-arg(I_hat)` modulo a full turn; select the principal branch in `(-pi,pi]`, preserve difference role and zero offset, and use an explicit wrapped subtraction if comparing phase values across the cut. A phase is neither a ratio nor a physical cycle tally. Counts, time-step indices and batch ordinals keep their separate meanings (`rule:vision:semantic-kinds-and-roles`). The bindings crate describes its semantic operation rules in `crates/bindings/README.md`.

`Measure` admits finite exact CAS constant expressions with checked positivity; rational scales are a special case, and offsets remain rational. The degree scale is exactly pi/180 and the turn scale is exactly 2*pi. Unchecked symbolic text, free-symbol dependence and nonpositive or unproved-positive definitions refuse; a decimal approximation never replaces the exact definition. The angle/frequency catalog registers radians, degrees and turns as ordinary descriptors and retains the checked full-cycle relation `omega=2*pi*f` without identifying the distinct frequency kinds. These contracts are implemented by commits `6148848` and `96680bc`, with source evidence in `crates/bindings/src/measure.rs`, the angle/frequency catalog and `crates/bindings/tests/integration/exact_scales.rs` and `frequency_relations.rs`; the root boundary records their exact/export distinction (`spec:nrz:descriptor-units-boundary`).

The exact prefixes mH and microfarad, coherent rad/s, Hz, seconds, ohms, joules and radians remain admitted. The values of f0, T and the nonzero radian phases contain pi while their measures have rational scale one; degrees and turns instead place pi in the measure map. Both are exact descriptor cases and both need numerical constant evidence when their required exported coefficient remains irrational. `crates/bindings/src/constants/model.rs::DescriptorConstant::export` still returns `UnsupportedExactConstantExport` for non-rational coefficients. Exact admission therefore does not claim a numerical pi evaluator or a completed degree-to-radian heap conversion.

## Matrix representations and the heterogeneous state · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-matrix`

**Argument (Normalization preserves meaning only with its maps)** · `arg:nrz:lc-resonance-normalization`

The physical domain axis is `(voltage,current)` and the codomain axis is `(voltage/time,current/time)`. The entry `1/C` has units `V/(A*s)`, `-1/L` has units `A/(V*s)`, and the diagonal zeros carry their own rate quotient meanings. The state is mixed-kind even when represented by a two-element float buffer: x binds both entries, while v and i bind their respective projections with checked equations `v=x[0]` and `i=x[1]`; the buffer has no unit of its own (`rule:nrz:descriptor-binding-checked-overlap`). `RegionContract::check` delegates homogeneous admission to `crates/bindings/src/admission/recipe.rs::HomogeneousRecipe::check`, which refuses heterogeneous entries and formal quotients. Richer checked shapes and maps do not yet supply executable heterogeneous bindings (`arg:nrz:project-review-homogeneous`). This closed recipe does not limit float storage: the descriptor-graph and region-bindings steps must retain per-entry/block meanings as CAS graph structure, while matrix checks numerical shapes, finite payloads, versions and companion agreement (`step:nrz:lc-resonance-descriptor-graph`), (`step:nrz:lc-resonance-region-bindings`), (`prin:nrz:descriptor-binding-float-heap`), (`rule:nrz:descriptor-binding-kernel-boundary`). Every representation below needs original physical checks before zero elimination and per-entry restoration of centers, radii and both covariance axes (`rule:nrz:descriptor-binding-restoration`). Normalization does not erase these requirements.

For the reference instance choose fixed positive references `S=diag(5 V,1/10 A)` and `t_ref=1/2000 s`. With `x=S*x_hat` and `tau=t/t_ref`, the dimensionless operator is `B=t_ref*S^(-1)*A*S=[[0,1],[-1,0]]`. Its eigenvalues are `+i,-i`; physical rates restore by division by `t_ref`, then angular frequency uses the radian relation. This is a similarity with one declared time scale. Arbitrary independent row/column scaling of a square array does not preserve eigenvalues. For varied inputs the same fixed references remain definitions and `B` changes; runtime quantities must not be smuggled into source-free descriptor constants. Other references must be explicit problem declarations, recorded recipe choices or checked exact derivations from fixed definitions (`rule:nrz:descriptor-binding-reference-scales`). Componentwise restored errors enter a named norm only after division by its declared references, with outward arithmetic (`rule:nrz:descriptor-binding-mixed-norm`). The energy metric `G=diag(C,L)` satisfies `A^T*G+G*A=0`, giving the physical skew-adjoint structure for positive `L,C`; a Euclidean norm of unscaled volts and amperes has no physical meaning (`rule:nrz:shape-eigenproblems`), (`rule:nrz:shape-norms-and-conditioning`).

**Decision (Reuse float kernels through checked LC lowerings)** · `dec:nrz:lc-resonance-matrix-route`

Use float blocks bound to Complex phasors and the positive two-state spectral recipe for the conjugate pair, with the bindings-owned representation and whole-family contract (`def:nrz:descriptor-binding-symbol-binding`), (`rule:nrz:descriptor-binding-family-representation`). This exposes all reference observables without making general Complex arithmetic, a general eigensolver or a certified integrator prerequisites. The block identity is an exact algebraic lowering obligation, while the spectral step has its own complete-root argument; an ordinary square-solve certificate cannot substitute for it. On the nominal state the normalized root equation is `lambda^2+1=0`. On the admitted positive parameter family, positivity of the product and the zero trace prove both purely imaginary roots with their multiplicities, and outward arithmetic encloses their rates. Inputs whose enclosures include zero or violate the original structural contract receive a specific refusal; a central matrix with the desired structure is insufficient for an unstructured perturbation family.

For phasors, `R(i*X)=[[0,-X],[X,0]]` is invertible exactly when `X` is nonzero. The below/above reference blocks therefore admit the existing Real solve argument after checked lowering, while the resonance block is exactly zero. Computing `Z=0` is a valid successful observable; an admittance/current solve there refuses, and phase is undefined. For an uncertain `X` spanning zero, do not choose a phase from its center or fill the unresolved angle with zero. A branch-aware phase recipe must establish a separated sign or return its explicit unavailable/domain outcome. Absolute tolerances and named physical reference scales remain meaningful at zero impedance; relative error against the zero value does not (`rule:vision:local-and-observable-budgets`).

The initial statistical acceptance can be exact without pretending nonlinear `L,C` propagation is exact. Keep `L,C` and sample frequencies fixed, and use one shared real source-voltage amplitude with mean `3 V` and variance `1/4 V^2`. The below/above imaginary currents are `s/75` and `-s/75`, with means `1/25,-1/25 A`, variances `1/22500 A^2` and cross-covariance `-1/22500 A^2`. This follows by multiplying the two exact gains by the same source variance. The resonance current row stays unavailable while its impedance row succeeds. Shared `L,C` uncertainty instead requires a declared joint model and nonlinear assurance: first-order sensitivities are local approximations unless accompanied by a separately proved deterministic remainder or direct enclosure (`rule:nrz:batch-source-covariance`). This statistical fixture belongs to the implementing acceptance work; the corpus's exact ideal inputs acquire no invented uncertainty.

## What the CAS can certify · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-cas`

**Argument (LC uses the negative-radicand mixed family)** · `arg:nrz:lc-resonance-cas-family`

Positive-root identities and strict nonzero evidence justify writing them as `+/-i/sqrt(L*C)`. Real coefficient declarations remain Real, and no ordering of a Complex unknown is used. The mathematical family is local to the positive base; it does not assert a solution at `L=0` or `C=0`, where the original equation is undefined.

Executable mixed-family evidence (`step:nrz:cas-mixed-families`) comes from `crates/cas/src/tests/family_solver.rs::mixed_quadratic_piece_replays` and `mixed_square_root_sign_pieces`, which preserve the unspecialized Real parameter, check the three signs and independently import the complete coverage witness. `family_solver/properties.rs` tests conjugation-fixed positive roots, required projection premises, refusal of a sibling sign, sampled-base replacement, changed family and whole-region widening. `family_solver/cover.rs` separately tests complete whole-family cover replay, pending-cover retention and omission of the zero stratum. `family_solver/placement.rs` rejects changed placement associations, missing arms and Complex order.
