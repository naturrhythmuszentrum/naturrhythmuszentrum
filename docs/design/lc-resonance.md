# LC resonance as the first physical target · `spec:nrz:lc-resonance`

## Choice and evidence boundary · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-scope`

**Decision (A bounded LC-first deliverable)** · `dec:nrz:lc-resonance-first`

LC resonance is the first reusable physical target because one small model connects exact energy exchange, mixed voltage/current coordinates, a Complex conjugate spectrum, phasor response, angle branches and the distinction between angular and cyclic frequency. The instance in (`prob:exemplars:lc-resonance`) admits rational or independently enclosed answers throughout. It exposes boundaries that a static truss or resistor solve cannot exercise, while its two-state algebra permits a narrowly certified first spectral operation. Existing truss/resistor acceptance remains regression evidence; changing the first physics target does not discharge the heterogeneous execution finding (`arg:nrz:project-review-homogeneous`).

This record proposes replacements for review bites 3–6 in (`sec:nrz:project-review-next`). Bites 1–2, cumulative replay reuse and transitive tower closure, retain their objectives, ownership, verification bars and order (`step:nrz:project-review-replay-bite`), (`step:nrz:project-review-zero-bite`). The engine plan and the review remain unchanged. This is data and design: the reference corpus establishes expected answers and descriptor checks, and the proposed numerical operations still require their own implementation and acceptance.

Source inspection is against `cf0c36f7ef27be3260ad92c04cc6d79b7d7af12f` on 2026-10-22: `crates/units/README.md`, `crates/matrix/README.md`, `crates/cas/README.md`, `tools/docgen/src/problem_schema.rs`, `crates/matrix/src/binding/operation.rs` and `crates/cas/src/tests/family_solver*`. ## Physical model and independent answers · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-model`

**Definition (Series one-port and autonomous state)** · `def:nrz:lc-resonance-model`

Take ideal positive `L` and `C` in series, with passive signs and the phasor convention `Re(V_hat exp(i*omega*t/radian))`. Define `a=omega/radian` as a derivative rate, so `Z=i*X`, `X=a*L-1/(a*C)` and `Y=1/Z` where `Z` is nonzero. For source-free motion let current charge the capacitor: `C*dv/dt=i`, `L*di/dt=-v`. The state `x=(v,i)` has operator `A=[[0,1/C],[-1/L,0]]` and characteristic equation `s^2+1/(L*C)=0`, where `s` is a Complex rate. A positive angular frequency is `omega0=radian/sqrt(L*C)`; `f0=omega0/(2*pi rad)` and `T=2*pi rad/omega0`. The usual coordinate shorthand is `omega=2*pi*f`; its physical meaning includes the full-cycle angle.

The reference uses `L=25 mH=1/40 H`, `C=10 microfarad=1/100000 F` and the separate autonomous initial state `v=5 V`, `i=0 A`. The impedance samples use `omega=1000,2000,4000 rad/s`. The driven frequency-domain relation and the source-free initial-value energy check are different experiments on the same ideal elements. Absence of declared input uncertainty is not proof that a manufactured element is exact (`prin:vision:guarantees-name-scope`).

## Units, kinds and pi · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-units`

**Rule (Three rate meanings and an angle difference)** · `rule:nrz:lc-resonance-kinds`

Angular frequency in rad/s and cyclic frequency in Hz both have time exponent minus one, but retain distinct named kinds. A state eigenvalue is a growth/rotation rate in `s^(-1)`, with a third rate kind; its imaginary magnitude becomes angular frequency only through the explicit radian factor. Neither descriptor equality nor ordinary same-kind measure conversion implements `omega=2*pi*f`. The relation must retain its full-cycle angle, source declarations and original operations. The corpus uses one-second and one-hertz exact references to state `f0=(1 s/T)*(1 Hz)` without an implicit kind cast. The phase is the angle difference `arg(V_hat)-arg(I_hat)` modulo a full turn; select the principal branch in `(-pi,pi]`, preserve difference role and zero offset, and use an explicit wrapped subtraction if comparing phase values across the cut. A phase is neither a ratio nor a physical cycle tally. Counts, time-step indices and batch ordinals keep their separate meanings (`rule:vision:semantic-kinds-and-roles`). The units crate describes its semantic operation rules in `crates/units/README.md`.

`Measure` currently accepts a positive rational scale and rational offset, and refuses symbolic or irrational scales as `UnsupportedMeasureScale`, as stated by the units crate's measure conversion rule in `crates/units/README.md` and the descriptor boundary (`spec:nrz:descriptor-units-boundary`). The exact prefixes mH and microfarad, coherent rad/s, Hz, seconds, ohms, joules and radians all fit. No irrational scale is needed for `omega0=2000 rad/s`, `sqrt(L/C)=50 ohm`, the rational reactances, eigenvalue components or energy. The values of `f0`, `T` and the two nonzero radian phases contain pi, but their measures have rational scale one: irrational coordinates are a numerical-expression boundary, not automatically a measure-scale failure. Degrees hit the scale boundary directly because `1 degree=(pi/180) rad`; turns do too because `1 turn=2*pi rad`. A proposed measure-level cycle-to-radian conversion hits pi as well and still needs the distinct-kind relation. A rational decimal approximation to pi must never enter an exact scale as if it were the same measure.

## Matrix representations and the heterogeneous state · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-matrix`

**Argument (Normalization preserves meaning only with its maps)** · `arg:nrz:lc-resonance-normalization`

The physical domain axis is `(voltage,current)` and the codomain axis is `(voltage/time,current/time)`. The entry `1/C` has units `V/(A*s)`, `-1/L` has units `A/(V*s)`, and the diagonal zeros carry their own rate quotient meanings. The state is mixed-kind even when represented by a two-element Real buffer. `RegionContract::check` currently requires one homogeneous concrete difference descriptor and refuses heterogeneous entries and formal quotients; richer checked shapes and maps do not supply executable storage (`arg:nrz:project-review-homogeneous`). Every representation below needs retained per-entry/block ownership, physical checks before zero elimination, and correct restoration of centers, radii and both covariance axes for the original state. Normalization does not erase this requirement.

For the reference instance choose fixed positive references `S=diag(5 V,1/10 A)` and `t_ref=1/2000 s`. With `x=S*x_hat` and `tau=t/t_ref`, the dimensionless operator is `B=t_ref*S^(-1)*A*S=[[0,1],[-1,0]]`. Its eigenvalues are `+i,-i`; physical rates restore by division by `t_ref`, then angular frequency uses the radian relation. This is a similarity with one declared time scale. Arbitrary independent row/column scaling of a square array does not preserve eigenvalues. For varied inputs the same fixed references remain definitions and `B` changes; runtime quantities must not be smuggled into source-free descriptor constants. The energy metric `G=diag(C,L)` satisfies `A^T*G+G*A=0`, giving the physical skew-adjoint structure for positive `L,C`; a Euclidean norm of unscaled volts and amperes has no physical meaning (`rule:nrz:shape-eigenproblems`), (`rule:nrz:shape-norms-and-conditioning`).

**Decision (Reuse Real kernels and certify the small missing operation)** · `dec:nrz:lc-resonance-matrix-route`

Use Real blocks for phasor response and the positive two-state spectral recipe for the conjugate pair. This exposes all reference observables without making general Complex arithmetic, a general eigensolver or a certified integrator prerequisites. The block identity is an exact algebraic lowering obligation, while the spectral step has its own complete-root argument; an ordinary square-solve certificate cannot substitute for it. On the nominal state the normalized root equation is `lambda^2+1=0`. On the admitted positive parameter family, positivity of the product and the zero trace prove both purely imaginary roots with their multiplicities, and outward arithmetic encloses their rates. Inputs whose enclosures include zero or violate the original structural contract receive a specific refusal; a central matrix with the desired structure is insufficient for an unstructured perturbation family.

For phasors, `R(i*X)=[[0,-X],[X,0]]` is invertible exactly when `X` is nonzero. The below/above reference blocks therefore admit the existing Real solve argument after checked lowering, while the resonance block is exactly zero. Computing `Z=0` is a valid successful observable; an admittance/current solve there refuses, and phase is undefined. For an uncertain `X` spanning zero, do not choose a phase from its center or fill the unresolved angle with zero. A branch-aware phase recipe must establish a separated sign or return its explicit unavailable/domain outcome. Absolute tolerances and named physical reference scales remain meaningful at zero impedance; relative error against the zero value does not (`rule:vision:local-and-observable-budgets`).

The initial statistical acceptance can be exact without pretending nonlinear `L,C` propagation is exact. Keep `L,C` and sample frequencies fixed, and use one shared real source-voltage amplitude with mean `3 V` and variance `1/4 V^2`. The below/above imaginary currents are `s/75` and `-s/75`, with means `1/25,-1/25 A`, variances `1/22500 A^2` and cross-covariance `-1/22500 A^2`. This follows by multiplying the two exact gains by the same source variance. The resonance current row stays unavailable while its impedance row succeeds. Shared `L,C` uncertainty instead requires a declared joint model and nonlinear assurance: first-order sensitivities are local approximations unless accompanied by a separately proved deterministic remainder or direct enclosure (`rule:nrz:batch-source-covariance`). This statistical fixture belongs to the implementing acceptance work; the corpus's exact ideal inputs acquire no invented uncertainty.

## What the CAS can certify · `sec:nrz:lc-resonance-cas`

**Argument (LC is the negative-radicand S4 piece)** · `arg:nrz:lc-resonance-cas-family`

Let `r=-1/(L*C)` on the Real base stratum `L>0,C>0`, retain the strict denominator obligations, and let `s` remain a Complex fibre unknown. Then `s^2-r=0` is the negative-radicand case of the worked `z^2-r` family (`rule:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-stratum-evidence`). A positive Real root `q=sqrt(1/(L*C))` supports the selected values `+i*q,-i*q`; checked selection, membership and the factor identity establish both roots and exclude any others on that stratum. Positive-root identities and strict nonzero evidence justify writing them as `+/-i/sqrt(L*C)`. Real coefficient declarations remain Real, and no ordering of a Complex unknown is used. The mathematical family is local to the positive base; it does not assert a solution at `L=0` or `C=0`, where the original equation is undefined.

The executable S4 evidence is `crates/cas/src/tests/family_solver.rs::mixed_quadratic_piece_replays` and `mixed_square_root_sign_pieces`, which preserve the unspecialized Real parameter, check the three signs and independently import the complete coverage witness. `family_solver/properties.rs` tests conjugation-fixed positive roots, required projection premises, refusal of a sibling sign, sampled-base replacement, changed family and whole-region widening. `family_solver/cover.rs` separately tests complete whole-family cover replay, pending-cover retention and omission of the zero stratum. `family_solver/placement.rs` rejects changed placement associations, missing arms and Complex order. These tests support exact existence, membership, multiplicity/coverage and their retained contexts; they do not certify an LC heap binding. The zero-stratum test matters for a whole `z^2-r` family, but is not an extra admissible LC input when `L,C` are strictly positive.

The available checkers can validate supplied exact algebraic LC obligations within their finite fragment and budgets. Automatic discovery for a newly assembled two-parameter LC request is a separate acceptance claim. In particular `solving/family/real_roots.rs::guard` looks for an explicit positive base guard formally equal to the radicand or its opposite; it does not automatically synthesize `1/(L*C)>0` merely from two separate input declarations. The LC adapter must retain checked positivity/definedness evidence and transport the original relation to the appropriate admitted piece, or report the missing producer step. It must prove the exact LC expected judgment, not merely inspect bytes proving the abstract `z^2-r` theorem. Bite 1's cumulative replay contract and bite 2's relation closure remain relevant to that transport; no new root degree or arbitrary ideal-completeness claim is needed.

CAS does not certify that a floating matrix represents the physical `A`, the numerical enclosure of an eigenvalue, a phasor argument branch, a trajectory or a pi-dependent numerical conversion. A selected algebraic root also supplies no global continuous/analytic branch; the represented implicit-function schema is unavailable in the reviewed implementation. The missing integration is an original-expression/shape binding plus a numerical spectral theorem, with fresh expected-judgment replay for the symbolic part and independent numerical verification for the heap part. CAS remains physics-free, units retains physical meanings, and no heap coordinate is sent to CAS to manufacture exact numerical evidence (`dec:nrz:matrix-core-cas-boundary`), (`arg:nrz:project-review-checker`).
