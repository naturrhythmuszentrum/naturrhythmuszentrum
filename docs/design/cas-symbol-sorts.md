# Real and complex symbol sorts · `spec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-contract`

This design belongs to owner NRZ, area nrz, and targets `73bc972cada7436893952350a1041747fcf69995`. It replaces the coefficient-interpretation proposal. The contract makes declaration sorts immutable and makes piecewise placement relative to an explicit, checked ambient space. Real unknowns are solved entirely in Real space; mixed complex calculations are piecewise families over their Real parameter base. Completeness of a solution on one piece and coverage of a larger requested domain are separate claims. It specifies later implementation, not a capability delivered by this documentation commit. The first combined acceptance is the six symbolic blind-cubic tests returning checked answers with independent oracles under unchanged `Limits::blind_cubic()`.

Spaces form a graph: a directed placement specifies how expressions of one declared space have pieces in a particular ambient. Real r alone is complete in Real and has no piecewise structure; in the Complex ambient of `r+z`, its placement has a real piece and is undefined elsewhere. The existing region, local-hypothesis, closure and cover machinery carries this ordinary incompleteness. There is no scalar inclusion map, value-conversion edge, redeclared symbol, round-trip certificate, `InterpretationCorrespondence` or `RealRestriction` route. The mixed semantics, AnyExact/RealExact presentation and retained-route preservation are settled decisions below; the remaining findings concern implementation evidence and capabilities.

## Ambient spaces and explicit placements · `sec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-space-graph`

**Decision (Piecewise placement is relative to the ambient)** · `dec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-partial-views`

An expression in its own declared space has no placement-induced piecewise structure: Real r alone is complete in Real. The ambient of each expression is an explicit checked fact, determined by its operator signature and its operands' declared spaces. The registered signatures give Real for Real-only arithmetic, Complex for Complex-only arithmetic, and Complex for mixed Real/Complex arithmetic. A checked `AmbientSpace` reference records this result; a caller cannot change it to retag an operand. An explicit ambient request must satisfy the same signatures and placement requirements. The original r node keeps Real ambient; its use in a Complex context has a separate native case node with Complex ambient and the Real-in-Complex placement. Sharing r between those uses does not make it intrinsically piecewise or mutate its declaration. For future spaces, multiple admissible ambients require an explicit choice or `Unresolved(AmbientSpaceAmbiguous)`; there is no implicit least upper bound or preferred parent.

Spaces are nodes, with directed edges of the form “A is placed as pieces of B”. Each edge has its own definition, including pieces, their region language, local binding signature, supported operation agreements and precisely which laws transfer. The graph is not a chain or a total order. No edge is inferred from names, numeric rank, shared laws, a reverse edge or a path through other spaces. Composition needs its own registered placement definition and checked agreement with the component pieces. The relation graph may have cycles, but syntax and proof dependencies remain acyclic. A mixed operator reads the selected edges from its operands' declared spaces into its checked ambient; an absent edge returns `Unresolved(SpacePlacementUnavailable)` identifying the operand space and ambient. This is a capability refusal, not permission to invent a conversion or reject an otherwise valid source declaration.

A placed expression is represented by the native case mechanism. Each arm associates a region in the named ambient with the original local expression and its registered placement. Its defined part is the union of those regions intersected with local value-definedness. A cover certificate is optional and separate. No implicit otherwise, zero value or totality claim fills a missing arm. This uses (`def:nrz:cas-piecewise-object`), (`dec:nrz:cas-piecewise-default`) and (`rule:nrz:cas-piecewise-local-hypotheses`), with the explicit local-space typing revision recorded below. It is not an unrestricted union of differently shaped values.

The absence of placement-induced cases does not totalize an operator's own partial domain. A prospective Natural n is complete in Natural; it becomes piecewise relative to Integer, Rational or Real only through an edge registered for that particular ambient. Even in Natural, `n-m` is incomplete where natural subtraction has no result: its operation domain would require `n>=m`. This is operation partiality in the same native case mechanism, not evidence that Natural is a field or that subtraction must silently choose Integer. Natural arithmetic and those edges are not designed or enabled in this lane.

**Definition (Space and placement contracts)** · `def:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-space-contract`

A `ScalarSpace` record names a closed, versioned space specification: coordinate/value grammar, operator signatures and partial domains, region/predicate vocabulary, and available algebraic-law schemas. It has no parent-rank or inherited-law field. A `SpacePlacement` record links an exact source space to an exact ambient and names its closed placement definition. A `ScalarPiece` instantiates one of that definition's pieces, linking the placement, ambient region and existing local binding frame. These records describe syntax; none is a theorem of membership, equality, nonemptiness or cover. Registrations are closed checker code and versioned contracts, not caller-supplied laws. These records are distinct from the existing distribution/test `Space` objects.

A further space must state which predicates are total, which membership procedures exist, which meets/unions/differences it supports, and how local hypotheses and definedness are checked. A further placement must supply its own finite piece representation, ambient region language, local binding and value-definedness rules, overlap obligations, and explicit operation agreements with their domains and law-transfer premises. It may require several pieces, or may not exist at all. Unknown membership stays unknown. Portable identities include the exact space and placement specification versions, so replay cannot substitute a different definition with the same printed name. An edge never promises a cover of its entire ambient.

Laws belong to spaces and transfer only where the chosen edge explicitly permits them. Real supplies ordered-field laws and the real root rules below; Complex supplies commutative-field, conjugation and complex existence rules, without order on arbitrary Complex values. A later noncommutative space receives no commutativity, factor-reordering, symmetric-polynomial, zero-product or inverse law merely from having Real or Complex pieces. A local Real proof can use Real laws on its own piece; a surrounding operation requires the ambient's laws and the edge's stated operation agreement. Solver capability checks name those exact requirements. No hypercomplex coordinates, arithmetic, placement or root algorithm is specified here, and different future ambients need not place Real in the same way.

**Definition (The Real-in-Complex placement)** · `def:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-real-placement`

This lane specifies one placement edge in full, Real into Complex. Its single piece is the real locus `ImagPart(q)=0` for a typed Complex location q. Its local binding frame has Real t and the incidence equations `RealPart(q)=t`, `ImagPart(q)=0`. The ambient region language is the existing Boolean/region language with defined Real and imaginary components, equality, local Real predicates and their checked meets and unions. Membership supplies the local Real binder through these equations; evaluating the original declaration at that local argument is same-Real substitution. It never substitutes q for a Real declaration or introduces a second global r. For a local expression e on Real domain D, the arm retains e and D, with support given by this incidence and D's predicates. The arm is complete wherever e is defined on D; there is no arm outside that support. Query locations are separate bound locations, not copied coefficient declarations.

The edge permits local zero, one, addition, negation, multiplication and defined reciprocal identities to act pointwise through the usual Complex component equations. Reciprocal transfer requires the local nonzero premise; strict arithmetic retains every operand's definedness. On this piece, the placed arm's Real component is the original Real e, its imaginary component is zero, and Complex conjugation fixes the arm. These are typed component/placement judgments, not cross-sort scalar equalities. Ordered comparisons remain Real statements in the local frame. These agreements transfer local field identities on the piece, not order to arbitrary Complex values, a global total value, or a global theorem from a local proof. Their applications expand to the registered component definitions and checked Real/Complex identities with the exact context and region; membership alone is not an algebraic proof.

A query proved outside all pieces returns `Refused(NoApplicablePiece)` with region-exclusion evidence; an undecided membership query returns `Unresolved(PieceMembershipPending)`. Absence of a value is neither malformed syntax nor a theorem that an equation has no roots. In mixed `r+z`, the real-locus restriction is on the ambient location for the Real operand r, not on the independent Complex coordinate z: z may have any Complex value. The equivalent family description uses Real r as a base coordinate and Complex z as a fibre coordinate. It describes precisely the defined piece; it does not totalize r over other Complex locations.

## Declarations and the base–fibre graph · `sec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-graph`

**Definition (Declaration identity and invariant sort)** · `def:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-declarations`

A scalar symbol is one declaration identified by its immutable header ancestry and declaration key, carrying an exact ScalarSpace identity. The executable spaces in this design are Real and Complex; the representation permits later registered spaces without changing declaration identity or inventing automatic promotions. A `Free` occurrence links to that declaration; a bound occurrence links to its exact binder slot and slot sort. Coordinate spaces reference those same declarations with a role, not an independently editable name/sort list. Rational and Gaussian coefficient constants remain exact arithmetic objects; they are not new classes of symbol declarations. Conservative header extension may append fresh declarations but may not shadow or change inherited sorts. Header field defaults cease to determine a symbol's sort: an explicitly typed declaration and an explicitly typed unknown space are authoritative.

Binding, simultaneous substitution, coordinate renaming, coefficient specialization and tuple admission require exact source-slot and image sorts. A Real declaration cannot receive a Complex expression, including one proved self-conjugate; a Complex declaration cannot receive a Real expression. A typed Complex literal whose value happens to be a real number remains an admissible Complex value. Projecting a Complex expression with `RealPart` produces a distinct Real value expression and does not change any declaration on which it depends. Such a projection does not satisfy a demand to replace the original Complex symbol by a Real symbol. Full source traversal retains dependencies even if later arithmetic cancels them.

Admission rejects `Free(r):Complex` for declared Real r, a Complex binder replacing a Real slot, a map that relabels a base coordinate as a fibre coordinate, a changed inherited declaration, a false claimed dependency partition, and mismatched headers, scopes or family references. These are `Invalid(SymbolSortMismatch)` or a more specific existing structural invalidity. Valid graph syntax whose solve request is outside a producer remains admissible and may return a named unresolved result. Admission supplies typing, not existence, sign, coverage or a homomorphism theorem.

Acyclic construction order is registered scalar spaces and placement definitions, declaration/binding frames and coordinate spaces, piece regions and ScalarPiece instances, base region and base, family, strata, local expressions and native cases, local contexts, problems and selections, solution sets/pieces, then applications and proofs. Placement definitions name their closed piece vocabulary, not later piece instances; ScalarPiece points to its earlier placement, region and frame, preventing a space/region cycle. Case arms introduce no hidden binders: their local frame and incidence premises are explicit existing binding/context structure. No base points to a theorem certifying itself. Structural keys contain the exact base/family and ambient/placement references, declaration identities, scope, operator and ordered roles; application keys additionally contain the full context, region and residuals. A family sharing expression syntax on two strata does not share the strata's proof authority.

The base includes all Real parameter dependencies of the original mixed expression; Complex parameters and unknowns are fibre coordinates. Unused ambient declarations may remain in the header but are not silently added to the source problem. A Real coefficient expression may depend on other Real expressions and prior Real selections. A Complex declaration under a constraint `ImagPart(z)=0` remains a fibre coordinate; the constraint neither moves it into B nor grants ordering of z. Base changes substitute Real coordinates by Real expressions with explicit definedness and stratum pullback; fibre substitutions preserve Complex slots. They are ordinary same-sort substitutions, never a round trip between unknown spaces.

## Mixed families and piecewise evidence · `sec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-families`

**Definition (Mixed expression as a family relation)** · `def:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-family-arithmetic`

For Real r and Complex z, the source expression `r + z` has checked ambient Complex and is a native incomplete piecewise expression relative to that ambient. Its Real base piece, obtained from the placement incidence, supplies the original r; on that piece a local FamilyExpression(Add) relates the result to r and z, and elsewhere the surrounding view has no arm. At a defined base point the value of r acts in the Complex fibre. The component equations are `Re(w)=r+Re(z)` and `Im(w)=Im(z)`; multiplication by r similarly gives `Re(w)=r Re(z)` and `Im(w)=r Im(z)`. These equations define the local Complex operation, with Real right-hand components and the same original declarations. The pointwise action is permitted; it neither converts r nor extends its expression to base locations where no piece is present.

The family syntax accepts arbitrary mixtures of declared Real and Complex symbols within the supported operator language. For several Real dependencies, instantiate the Real-in-Complex incidence for each needed coordinate in the ambient product frame. Native case composition forms the meet of these operand regions, retaining each explicit operand/piece association, reached-guard and strict-value definedness, and overlap compatibility. The Real base is the corresponding local coordinate product, not a second list of retyped coefficients. It does not demand a cover of an enclosing space before using a local piece. Extend each operator's descriptor, source traversal, binding, substitution and local evaluation contract consistently; unsupported mathematical operators keep their specific capability boundary. A FamilyExpression is a local body, not a replacement case or an ordinary scalar operand for an old ScalarAmbient map. Family targets check underlying local sorts, space laws, edge-specific agreements and exact base/fibre roles. A base-only expression stays Real. A formal coefficient polynomial can retain Real expressions on the base and Complex fibre variables with all localization conditions; sharing arithmetic scratch never erases their roles.

**Decision (Mixed arithmetic is ordinary piecewise incompleteness)** · `dec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-fibre-semantics`

Mixed evaluation has no semantic approval gate. Use the existing region, local-hypothesis, closure, case-definedness and optional-cover mechanisms for the real piece of `r + z`, exactly as for other incomplete piecewise expressions. Selection on that piece checks membership and the local operator's definedness; a query proved outside all pieces returns NoApplicablePiece, not a sort error. A query whose membership is undecided remains unresolved rather than assuming an absent piece. For any other source/ambient pair, the same case rules use that pair's explicit placement definition if one exists; they infer neither a placement nor a universal Real piece. Local operator rules, space laws and edge-specific agreements state how values act at points of a piece; no coefficient embedding, bridge certificate or changed declaration is introduced.

**Rule (Stratum facts and exact family coverage)** · `rule:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-stratum-evidence`

Every complex piece is proved under the local membership hypothesis for its base stratum S_i and, when necessary, a fibre coefficient cell T_ij lying over S_i. Its complete-solution claim says that the reported set equals the original problem's solutions for every admissible parameter in T_ij, with no further roots there. This claim needs no cover of B or of the remaining fibre parameter domain. If a whole-domain answer is requested, separately prove `B = union S_i` and `for every p in S_i, union_j T_ij(p) = D_i(p)`, where D_i is the exact declared fibre coefficient domain; apply the existing cover elimination to the closed piece claims. Closing a local context retains its region. “Exactly on its stratum” names the precise theorem domain; it does not assert that the same formula is false outside it. Sharing a formula across strata needs separate proofs or a checked merge.

Reuse shared regions, unordered associations and explicit covers from (`dec:nrz:cas-piecewise-graph-representation`), (`dec:nrz:cas-piecewise-default`) and (`def:nrz:cas-piecewise-object`). Reuse local hypotheses, guarded closure, elimination and replay from (`rule:nrz:cas-piecewise-local-hypotheses`), (`rule:nrz:cas-piecewise-conditional-closure`), (`rule:nrz:cas-piecewise-case-elimination`) and (`rule:nrz:cas-piecewise-replay`). Their concrete context and residual rules are (`def:nrz:cas-piecewise-design-context-model`), (`rule:nrz:cas-piecewise-design-residuals`) and (`tab:nrz:cas-piecewise-design-case-rules`). Solving's original-region branches and total predicate splits are already specified in (`constr:nrz:cas-solving-regions`), (`def:nrz:cas-solving-design-regions`) and (`tab:nrz:cas-solving-design-certificates`).

Implementation still needs the explicit ambient/placement and base/fibre structure, projection-aware restriction through the existing region rules, the settled local operator definitions, symbolic Real sign reasoning, and producers of the required root-count and piecewise proofs. The one-coordinate rational affine decision procedure in (`tab:nrz:cas-piecewise-design-predicates`) is not a multivariate sign decomposition or cylindrical algebraic decomposition. A finite RealSigns split of a proved-total polynomial still proves a cover in any finite Real parameter dimension without solving whether every child is nonempty. Keep an undecided child; remove it only with an emptiness proof. Partial guards do not acquire a false branch at undefined points. The existing RealSigns schema already expresses the required trichotomy; its meaning need not change.

**Rule (Local fibre hypotheses and scalar-space laws)** · `rule:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-local-fibre-hypotheses`

A fibre proof may cite `r>0`, `r<s`, `r=0`, nonvanishing, and definedness only through the exact base-local hypothesis and its projection-membership dependency. It may use positivity to justify a base reciprocal or Real square root, and use their resulting family coefficient identities under the checked local operator definitions. It may use conjugation fixing base-dependent coefficients through a checked family-conjugation law. It may not order a Complex coordinate, infer that a Complex coefficient is real, assume positivity on a sibling stratum, or treat a formal nonzero rational function as nonzero at every base point. Realness of a base coefficient is structural in its Real declaration; a derived family self-conjugacy statement is still an application citing that structure and the operator law.

For the mixed example `z²-r=0`, partition B into `r<0`, r=0 and `r>0`. The fibre answer has two nonzero opposite roots on each nonzero stratum and a repeated zero root on the middle stratum; on `r>0` a family-conjugation proof can certify both selected values fixed by conjugation, while on `r<0` a real positive-square certificate for −r supports the corresponding imaginary components. A reader checks each selected relation, its existence and product, all three base cells and the singleton, rather than trusting a “real parameter” flag. Omitting r=0 fails a claim of whole-base coverage; it leaves the completed nonzero pieces valid on their own strata. Complex coefficient guards such as `c=0` belong to T_ij, not to the base; any real condition involving `Re(c)` remains a predicate on the total family unless a separate projection theorem establishes a base-only condition.

Three outcomes stay distinct. A complete solution on a named piece is a proved result, including a proved empty root set on that piece. A query proved outside the expression's support returns NoApplicablePiece. If a requested whole-domain answer lacks a proved base or fibre cover, retain the outstanding regions in `unresolved_regions` with `BaseCoveragePending` or `FibreCoveragePending`, alongside the already complete piece claims. A pending cover does not revoke those claims or prove a gap. A cover is not demanded merely to select an existing piece. A proved empty coefficient cell may be pruned locally with evidence. An empty requested domain follows the existing domain-outcome contract. Overlapping pieces need equal solution sets on overlaps; numbered scalar root branches additionally require compatibility if advertised as one single-valued function. A family of root sets does not imply a global continuous numbering, especially around complex discriminant loci.

## Real unknowns and complete real answers · `sec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-real-route`

**Definition (Complete roots on a piece, separate base coverage)** · `def:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-piece-completeness`

For a Real coefficient stratum S and the original admissible Real unknown region U, a complete piece answer R proves, for every parameter p in S and every x in U(p), that x belongs to R(p) exactly when the original equation and denominator/domain conditions hold. Thus it certifies all real roots on that piece and no further real roots there, including multiplicities when requested. Every local sign, unit, existence and count premise must be discharged or explicitly retained as an assumption in the stated context. No cover of the larger coefficient base B is a premise of this local completeness claim. A separate cover of B by completed strata is required only to claim the whole-base answer.

A count-one cell proves completeness within that cell; it does not by itself prove completeness over U. To claim the complete root set on S across U, include the other cells, endpoint singletons and exterior/exclusion counts. Unsearched strata or uncovered regions remain explicit while each finished piece retains its checked no-further-roots conclusion. The six acceptance fixtures request the whole original domain, so their acceptance still requires all degree and sign strata and the separate base cover; optional coverage for a local result does not weaken that bar.

**Procedure (Real cubic strata and construction)** · `alg:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-real-cubic`

1. Preserve the original Real unknown x, Real coefficient declarations, equation, admissible domain and original denominator exclusions. Derive the actual polynomial by the existing complete-source discipline. Split leading coefficients by total zero/nonzero conditions before division: a≠0 is cubic; a=0,b≠0 is quadratic; a=b=0,c≠0 is linear; a=b=c=0,d≠0 is a certified empty set; a=b=c=d=0 is the original-domain identity family. Zero coefficients are facts on strata, not assignments that erase declarations.
2. On a≠0 derive `x=t-b/(3a)` by exact polynomial composition. Its inverse is a same-Real substitution. The computed depressed coefficients are `p=(3ac-b²)/(3a²)` and `q=(2b³-9abc+27a²d)/(27a³)`. Derive these identities, retain a≠0 as the unit premise, and form the Real expression `D=(q/2)²+(p/3)³`. D has the opposite sign from the conventional cubic discriminant `Disc=-108a⁴D`; never interchange the two sign conventions.
3. Use `TotalPredicateSplit::RealSigns` to cover the cubic stratum by `D>0`, D=0 and `D<0`, with D's definedness justified by a≠0. An equivalent total polynomial numerator may be used only with a sign-equivalence proof and the positive denominator factor. No numerical sample chooses a branch. Each child retains its local membership provenance.
4. On `D>0` choose the positive Real square root s of D, and the unique Real cube roots u and v of `-q/2+s` and `-q/2-s`. Their independently proved equations give `(uv)³=(-p/3)³`; injectivity of the real cube gives `uv=-p/3` without division by u or v. Thus `t=u+v` satisfies the depressed polynomial by exact expansion. A real root-count certificate proves it is the sole real root, so the answer contains one Real tuple and a discharged no-further-real-roots claim. The selected square/cube roots and every intermediate have Real sorts.
5. On D=0 and p=q=0 return t=0 with multiplicity three. Otherwise D=0 implies `p<0` and q≠0; with `u=cuberoot(-q/2)`, the factor identity is `(t-2u)(t+u)²`. Return the two distinct Real roots, certify u≠0 and multiplicities, and derive complete roots on that stratum from the factorization. Do not divide by p or q without their branch unit proofs.
6. On `D<0`, `p<0`. Choose `s=sqrt(-p/3)>0`. Define three Real cells `(-infinity,-s)`, `(-s,s)` and `(s,infinity)`. The identities `f(-s)=q+2s³`, `f(s)=q-2s³` and `D<0` prove the first endpoint value positive and the second negative. Each cell has exactly one root; neither critical point is a root. Select those three roots with their actual cell certificates, then translate them back to x. These are exact real algebraic answers; no complex radical is constructed.
7. For the quadratic stratum split its Real discriminant into negative, zero and positive. Negative gives certified zero real roots, zero gives one double Real root, positive gives two Real square-root expressions. Linear and constant cases use exact identities and units. Preserve all original domain and denominator checks on every candidate and certify every excluded root. A rational equation is not replaced by its cleared polynomial without those checks.

This producer uses generic affine composition, polynomial division, sign splits and exact root relations. Its real radical proposal can be derived by enumerating sums of two real root variables, expanding their degree-n power, comparing coefficients and eliminating the product; at degree three this computes the quadratic for u³ and v³. The checker receives the resulting equations and existence proofs, never a stored Cardano answer. The fixed real classification and root-count strategy are an explicit extension of the search policy, requiring the concept revision below; the current Complex resolvent audit is not silently claimed to describe that new route. Any proposed implementation that instead hardcodes the displayed cubic formula must declare that policy change and cannot pass the existing blindness audit by renaming it.

**Definition (Replayable real root counts and cells)** · `def:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-real-count`

Add `RealRootCount(relation, coefficient_stratum, cells, counts, exterior_counts)` and a separate root-existence application for each count-one cell. There is no assertion that a base-dependent count-one selection is a radical or a globally analytic branch. A RealCell is structural syntax with Real endpoint expressions or signed infinity, openness, header and scope. Finite endpoints must already be defined independently of the root being selected. Their order and non-root status are premises. The root selection may name the cell only after the relation and cell exist; its existence proof consumes the count, not its own `RootValue`. A joint selection of the three ordered cells has existence because each is inhabited and the cells are disjoint, with no assertion about complex roots.

The finite count checker reconstructs a Sturm chain for the square-free part of the actual Real polynomial. It checks the polynomial/derivative identity, every signed division remainder and strict degree drop, leading units on the stratum, and the terminal gcd or constant. Positive rescalings require positivity evidence. It independently reconstructs signs at all finite endpoints and at both infinities from leading signs and parity; zero intermediate chain values are omitted according to Sturm's rule. Finite endpoints that are roots require explicit singleton treatment and one-sided counts, not ordinary non-root endpoint subtraction. Variation differences certify counts in disjoint cells, and their total plus singleton/exterior counts certifies all roots on the original Real line. Gcd and derivative identities separately transfer multiplicities.

For the depressed cubic with p≠0, an independently checkable chain is `f=t³+pt+q`, `f'=3t²+p`, `-(2p/3)t-q`, `-27D/p²`; these are illustrative identities for the checker, not trusted table entries. Branch p=0 separately, and handle D=0 with the actual gcd. When `D<0` the infinity variation difference is three, and the critical cells each have count one. When `D>0` it is one; the radical candidate supplies membership and existence, and the count excludes every other Real root. Order follows from the cell endpoints, not from an attempt to sort Complex values. The checker can also use monotonicity and the intermediate value theorem as a separately registered real theorem, but the first implementation chooses Sturm counting and does not add two competing proof formats.

Real sign evidence is a bounded ordered-field derivation: exact identities, rational signs, product/sum sign rules, squares and even powers, trichotomy hypotheses, and the defining sign/equation of prior Real square or odd roots. Its closed rule inventory includes order reflection by a positive odd integer power, checked with the actual exponent and endpoints; this supplies the cube-injectivity step and may conclude an equality predicate. The rule is a declared real theorem, not a consequence of an unproved radical convention. In particular `(2s³)²-q²>0` and `2s³>0` imply `-2s³<q<2s³` on `D<0`. A producer-supplied endpoint sign vector is not authority. Every sign, unit and selection dependency is replayed. Higher-degree symbolic sign search may remain unresolved; this does not weaken the complete degree-at-most-three acceptance requirement.

The trusted new mathematical boundary is the real closed field root-count theorem with its checked signed-remainder premises, plus the explicitly enumerated ordered-field sign rules. The existing `RealOddRoot` and `PositiveRealSquareRoot` meanings remain unchanged; their producer gains same-Real symbolic coefficient evaluation and sign evidence. Viète supplies exact coefficient relations and factor checks as in (`arg:nrz:cas-solving-resolvent-identities`), while the realness work in (`constr:nrz:cas-solving-realness`) supplies the discipline of proved existence and actual selected values. The landed Complex formal-frame closure cannot be reused as proof that a cubic splits into three Real roots on `D>0`. Real existence comes from odd-root theorems or counts; any real Viète closure must be anchored in those actual existing roots.

**Decision (Real presentation and complex dependencies)** · `dec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-real-contract`

The accepted Real symbolic fixture request becomes `AnyExact`, with checked `RealExact` presentation: Real radicals on the one-root and applicable degenerate strata, and Real cell-selected algebraic values on the three-root stratum. The fixture currently asks for `Radicals`; changing that request is a visible contract revision, not merely reversing its refusal assertion. Keep `Radicals`' existing meaning. A separate request demanding only real radicals on the casus irreducibilis stratum retains proved coverage at `AnyExact` and reports `RequestedRealRadicalsUnavailable` for presentation; it does not report that no further roots can be determined. This presentation distinction is settled: an isolated algebraic value is not called a radical, and no trigonometric extension is required for this acceptance.

A Real-unknown problem with a reachable Complex declaration in its original equation, domain, coefficients or denominator inventory returns `Unresolved(RealProblemHasComplexSymbol)` before orbit work. This includes `Re(c)` and `c-c` when c is declared Complex; the first route deliberately has no certified exception. The expression can remain well-typed and meaningful, but the requested real producer does not prove the simultaneous real/imaginary reduction. A future exception would need an explicit theorem reducing the original predicate to real equations while preserving all original dependencies and domains. It cannot retag c, assign it a Real value, or invoke Complex solving followed by restriction. This is a capability boundary, not a statement that such real equations are mathematically unsolvable.

## Complex unknowns in their declared fibres · `sec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-complex-route`

**Rule (Complex coefficient algebra and scoped evaluation)** · `rule:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-complex-algebra`

For a pure Complex problem, keep the original fraction algebra and its Complex coordinate frame, use a singleton Real base as indexing metadata without a placement or Cases wrapper, and run the existing degree-generic translation, orbit, resolvent, symmetric reduction, linked selection and inverse-transform machinery. In particular `radicals/roots.rs::unity` already builds over the actual source algebra; do not replace it by a universal coefficient domain or a copy of the parameter declarations. A primitive unity and every radical have Complex root relations and explicit existence. Generic Complex coefficients have zero/nonzero strata, not Real discriminant sign strata. A ground real witness optimization is permissible only with its actual already-defined Real constant relation; it never changes a symbol's sort or solves a Real-unknown problem via Complex space.

For mixed inputs, the same machinery operates on the local family coefficient algebra over B and on T_ij, with its checked Complex ambient and Real-in-Complex placement. Base coefficients are retained as base-dependent coefficient expressions; Complex coefficients are fibre coordinates. Every polynomial, selected generator and proof retains F and its current base/fibre region. The evaluator implements the settled piecewise decision in (`dec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-fibre-semantics`); there is no pending semantic approval. On a base stratum, fixed-field facts can simplify conjugation of base coefficients, but cannot impose sign or realness on a fibre coefficient. General mixed syntax is an ordinary family case; producer refusals concern a specific missing theorem or operator, never the mere presence of two sorts.

Add a finite `CoefficientEvaluation` object when a nontrivial same-sort specialization is needed. It links the original coordinate space, exact target space or family, ordered slot bindings, localization and region. Each binding targets the slot's declared sort, and family base/fibre roles remain exact. Its `coeff.evaluate` application checks all image definedness, actual denominator nonvanishing and selected-root existence before evaluating the finite polynomial support. No proof of injectivity is inferred for a specialization. Canonical identity maps remain the narrow exact-Free case; finite evaluation is a separate constructor, not a weakened interpretation of those maps. A Real root coefficient such as `-q/2+sqrt(D)` can therefore be evaluated in Real without projecting or copying any Complex symbol.

In the complex path, normalize a coefficient only in its actual algebra or through an explicit same-sort evaluation whose support and units replay. `maps::canonical` follows true tower parents; its fallback `maps::constant` requires a scalar, so an unrelated symbolic fraction cannot be smuggled through it as a constant. `read::ground` accepts only Rational/Gaussian ground domains. Preserve the formal-root frame during exact Viète substitution, and erase it only after the entire conclusion, conditions and maps are root-independent. Extend scope traversal for any newly supported attached syntax with explicit dependencies; deleting an attachment or a denominator is never an optimization. These laws address real implementation boundaries without claiming that each is the recorded five-test failure site.

A Complex answer is complete on a coefficient piece after reconstruction of the original polynomial as its nonzero leading factor times all root factors, independent membership of each tuple, original denominator exclusions and selected-root existence on that piece. Completeness across the whole requested coefficient domain additionally requires its separate stratum cover. Keep `ProductCompleteness`, `RootSelectionRefinement`, `TotalPredicateSplit`, `ExtensionUnit` and `PermutationOrbitCoverage` as mathematical claims with their existing meanings. Conjugate choices or named radical branches do not substitute for linked selection compatibility. The five tests must replay their actual Coverage and Presentation against a separately reconstructed original problem; merely returning three expressions does not supply the missing capability.

## Bounded sort routing · `sec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-preflight`

**Procedure (Complete dependency check before expensive search)** · `alg:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-routing`

1. After the original-source receipt and ordinary admission, traverse the semantic dependency DAG of the original problem, including all guards, domains, denominators, selected-root relations, coefficient maps, family links and inactive source branches. Resolve each declaration once by qualified identity. Validate header declarations, but include only original expression/evaluation dependencies in the support; an unused Complex declaration elsewhere in the header does not itself trigger the Real producer refusal. Reconstruct each operator's ambient from the registered signature and operand spaces; check the selected placement definitions and each local arm's exact binding signature. Build the Real-base/Complex-fibre/unknown partition for these routes and compare stored ambient, sort and role claims with the reconstructed facts.
2. An exact declaration/binder/map sort contradiction or false stored role/ambient is sufficient for `Invalid(SymbolSortMismatch)` or `Invalid(AmbientSpaceMismatch)`, naming the offending edge and expected/actual spaces, before orbit enumeration, polynomial reduction or root search. A local arm matching its registered placement signature is valid; it is not a cross-sort scalar substitution. Missing placement or ambiguous ambient in a source request returns SpacePlacementUnavailable or AmbientSpaceAmbiguous, before constructing a placed expression. A well-typed Real unknown with any original Complex declaration instead gets RealProblemHasComplexSymbol. Point membership is a separate metered region operation: outside-piece queries return NoApplicablePiece, not a structural invalidity. None is the aggregate UnsupportedFragment at exact coefficients.
3. Route a Real unknown with Real-only dependencies to the real producer, a Complex unknown with no Real dependencies to the singleton-base Complex producer, and a Complex unknown with Real dependencies to the piecewise family producer. There is no mixed-semantics gate. A later registered space requires a producer with its exact law requirements; a placement alone does not enable a solver. A capability not decided by this scan continues to its producer; unsupported degree, missing unit, unavailable presentation and undecided coverage retain their specific later reasons. Symbolic fractions and incomplete piece covers are not themselves sort refusals.
4. Freeze the resulting routing descriptor as non-authoritative request metadata. Mathematical certificates retain the actual structural references and recheck them on fresh replay; they do not trust a producer's route flag. Typed syntax admission and supplied-certificate replay remain available independently of producer scheduling.

Let N, E, K and L be reached record count, semantic edge count, declaration count and inspected key/payload bytes. With a precharged visited set and declaration index, the scan is O(N+E+L+K log K) work and O(N+K) scratch; if canonical indexing has already been validated, lookup can reuse it with charged comparisons. Count all comparisons and bytes, not only nodes. Use the existing Work, Depth, vertices/edges and staged-payload limits; no new unbounded cache or separate unmetered pass. It performs zero coefficient operations, zero GCD steps, zero orbit permutations and zero tower reductions. A budget failure returns Exhausted, not a sort assertion. The implementation pins an exact work bound for each fixture and a cap-minus-one refusal; this design does not invent a measured constant for an unimplemented walker.

**Argument (Preservation and its precise scope)** · `arg:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-route-preservation`

For the retained Real/Complex routes, the preflight rejects violated declaration/binder/map/role/ambient invariants or the expressly unsupported Real-with-Complex producer case; their own-space or registered Real-in-Complex use supplies every needed placement. Every presently successful Complex ground case has no mismatched declarations and an empty Real base; every successful Complex symbolic linear or coefficient-stratum route has the same exact Complex keys in its initial map. Such requests pass, retain the old route and all its proof obligations, and are never rejected because a later coefficient primitive is incomplete. Existing real same-sort identities and supplied real root certificates remain replayable under the new current format. A valid request that the scan cannot classify receives no new negative theorem; it proceeds to capability handling. This is a structural preservation argument, backed by differential tests, not a runtime speed guarantee.

**Decision (Preserve retained declared-space routes)** · `dec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-preservation-decision`

Every retained declared-space producer passes the sort preflight when its existing declaration/slot invariants hold. This preservation scope is accepted. The RealRestriction supplied-certificate route is withdrawn, with no reader, alias or reinterpretation; its removal is a deliberate current-contract replacement. A former transport-convention use of Real symbols in Complex solving becomes an ordinary placed family expression under the new contract. Record the replacement in the concept/design revisions and test the retained ground, symbolic linear, coefficient-stratum and real certificate routes independently of solver availability.

Factored guards, shared square-root evidence and generic polynomial division should avoid the orbit and compositum costs of the Complex route. No new numerical speedup or headroom is asserted.

## Remaining implementation findings · `sec:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-findings`

**Open Question (Evidence and capabilities still needed)** · `open:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-review-items`

No semantic decision remains open for the owner in this design. The ambient-relative space graph and its explicit Real-in-Complex placement settle mixed arithmetic; per-piece completeness does not require a whole-base cover; the Real fixture requests AnyExact with RealExact; every retained declared-space producer passes preflight and RealRestriction is removed without a reader or alias. These decisions are specified above and are not gates awaiting another approval. Natural and hypercomplex examples constrain the interface only; they do not claim new supported spaces.

Three implementation findings remain. The first failing primitive of the five Complex runs is unlocalized by the supplied aggregate diagnostic and static reading, so the first-failure-diagnosis step (`step:nrz:cas-first-failure-diagnosis`) must locate it before calling a repair sufficient. Symbolic multivariate sign production beyond the existing affine predicate support is missing; the bounded sign/count rules above specify the required degree-three capability without claiming a general decomposition algorithm. Complete successful generation and replay costs are unmeasured, so unchanged-budget acceptance must measure them. A sort preflight or family wrapper alone does not solve these gaps. Any additional arithmetic defect requires its demonstrated law and regression, or an explicit design finding.

The mathematical identities and count requirements are independently checkable as described above. This design uses the supplied local repository and measurements and claims no newly measured solver capability or successful cost. The documentation gate checks its fixed point, labels, format and commit; implementation acceptance requires all six symbolic tests plus the mixed piecewise laws under unchanged limits. **Definition (Integer declaration and result graph)** · `def:nrz:cas-symbol-sorts-integer-graph`

`AtomicSort::Integer` is a value-sort payload on exact header declarations, binder slots and expressions, alongside the distinct `AtomicSort::Scalar` branch; it is not a registered `ScalarSpace`. An Integer expression links to its exact header and original operands but has no `CheckedAmbient` edge. A native Integer case links its declared result sort, domain and unordered associations; each association links an Integer value under the same scope.
