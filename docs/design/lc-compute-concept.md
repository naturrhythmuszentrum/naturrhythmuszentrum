# LC compute space and the optimized naive reference · `spec:nrz:lc-compute-concept`

**Principle (A computation reference, not a measured baseline)** · `prin:nrz:lc-compute-reference`

This concept supplies the problem and cost analysis behind (`claim:nrz:lc-closing-naive-model`), (`claim:nrz:lc-closing-overhead-budget`), (`claim:nrz:lc-closing-overhead-tracking`). It describes an optimized uncertified binary64 calculation of the same requested numerical selections, with no implementation or measurement. It prepares the overhead-tracking design; it neither sets acceptance tolerances nor closes measured claims. The completion authority remains (`sec:nrz:lc-closing-register`). Mathematical domain, existence, alias completeness and ordered source quantifiers remain those of (`sec:nrz:lc-relation`), (`spec:nrz:lc-evaluation-design`); a floating reference has no authority to certify them.

## The problem space · `sec:nrz:lc-compute-space`

**Definition (Cell, instance and materialized selection)** · `def:nrz:lc-compute-cell`

An instance index i names the complete tuple of finite or open instance axes. A numerical output cell is (i,j,b), including a requested sample j and a requested exact branch or cycle b when present, as in (`def:nrz:lc-evaluation-shapes`). N counts demanded original instances; J and B denote actual requested sample and branch multiplicities; K=N*J*B counts dense numerical selections. Irregular membership uses actual occupied selections, not padded capacity. An original instance has one family outcome even when several alternative cells represent it (`rule:nrz:lc-evaluation-alternative-masks`). A cell is not a descriptor, a feasible oscillator or one floating operation. Every cost quotient names N or K; an infinite alias family remains a lazy exact domain with finite requested numerical selections.

**Definition (Axes and input families)** · `def:nrz:lc-compute-families`

The illustrative grid ranks are one through four: a varying L axis; L by C; L by C by query-time window; and L by C by window by threshold. An axis may index window endpoints rather than point time. Threshold varies the predicate, not the oscillator. These examples do not impose a rank limit or make the physical sources independent (`def:nrz:lc-grid-inputs`). Independent positive rectangles allow monotone endpoint formulas; ordinary positive nonsquare L*C uses the same root formula without a rational-square gate (`sec:nrz:lc-grid-frequency`). An affine lattice describes lifted phase or integer aliases, such as delta_x+2*pi*k, with owned faces and complete integer domains (`def:nrz:lc-evaluation-lattice-recipe`). A Cartesian index layout alone supplies neither an affine physical law nor independent uncertainty. Arbitrary arrived arrays retain their charged scan and source association.

**Definition (Questions and required combinations)** · `def:nrz:lc-compute-questions`

The required vocabulary comprises natural rate/frequency/period, forward lifted or wrapped phase, phase-to-time members and numbering/tails, observations-to-rate/product/initial-phase recovery, inverse L or C with known element or positive amplitude/energy scale, energy/amplitudes and calibrated future horizons. Closing covers every question in (`sec:nrz:lc-grid-questions`) on rational-square controls and ordinary nonsquare inputs, both cell and region decisions, boundaries, aliases, correlated supports, rest and exhausted refinement (`req:nrz:lc-grid-benchmark`). Required initial native recipes and the charged fallback boundary are (`dec:nrz:lc-evaluation-initial-subset`). A region answer is certified yes only with the relevant complete coverage or feasible witness, no with the relevant exclusion or counterexample, and undecided when obligations remain, preserving quantifier order (`def:nrz:lc-grid-predicate`). It cannot be replaced by counting nominal comparisons. Fixed-window and observed-domain stream questions keep their distinct incidence and absence laws (`def:nrz:lc-stream-standing-question`), (`rule:nrz:lc-stream-decision-scope`).

**Caveat (State and extension boundaries)** · `cav:nrz:lc-compute-extensions`

An instantaneous supplied/restored state supports source-free energy, while publishing voltage/current trajectories from a forward phase is a named extension under (`claim:nrz:lc-closing-excludes-trajectories`). Its arithmetic is recorded in (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-state`) to expose the transcendental cost without adding it to phase closing. General atan2, clock drift, damping, topology variants and multiple modes retain (`sec:nrz:lc-closing-boundaries`). The existing driven impedance/current/sign experiment is required in its own reusable-plan scope (`rule:nrz:lc-evaluation-driven-boundary`); it does not convert the autonomous phase question into a driven trajectory problem. Arbitrary nonlinear joint elimination and quantifier alternation are not covered by a fixed per-cell floating formula.

**Definition (Outputs and scale)** · `def:nrz:lc-compute-outputs`

A full value map stores q scalar outputs at each demanded selection. A decision map stores predicate outcomes and their association, with certification adding semantic evidence and distinct masks. Aggregates include counts, extrema and scoped universal/existential conclusions. First/last queries return an original index and its condition outcome; serial early exit examines E<=K selections, while the worst case examines K and parallel blocks may inspect extra selections. No O(1) first/last claim follows without a monotonicity/search law. Populations from 10³ through 10⁹ and beyond change output volume, not the LC formulas: a single binary64 plane occupies 8 KB, 8 MB and 8 GB at 10³, 10⁶ and 10⁹ cells respectively, using decimal bytes. Streaming can bound live numerical residence independently of population; keeping the entire map requires its full external or resident storage (`rule:nrz:lc-grid-streaming`). The actual billion-cell run remains (`claim:nrz:lc-closing-scale-measurement`).

## Naive per-cell computation · `sec:nrz:lc-compute-naive`

**Convention (Operation inventory and floating domain)** · `conv:nrz:lc-compute-operations`

Write an inventory as (A,M,F,D,S), counting scalar additions/subtractions, multiplications, fused multiply-adds, divisions and square roots; an F is one instruction and two conventional flops. Negation and absolute-value bit operations, comparisons, integer/floor operations and loads/stores are reported separately. Constants half, two, turn=2*pi and its reciprocal are imported once. The reference uses finite normal binary64 operands, positive separated divisors and no overflow in the stated evaluation order; exceptional magnitudes need scaling and their additional counts. FMA is permitted with its usual single rounding. These are numerical reference assumptions, not new admission restrictions on the engine. A whole-region endpoint answer is charged for every endpoint or candidate face actually evaluated, not priced as its center alone.

**Calculation (Frequency and forward phase)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-phase`

Let P=L*C, tau=sqrt(P), a=1/tau and phi=fma(a,t,alpha), with coherent coordinates from (`def:nrz:lc-evaluation-symbols`). Computing a costs (0,1,0,1,1); computing phi costs (0,1,1,1,1). Requested f=a/turn adds one multiplication by the fixed reciprocal, T=turn*tau adds one multiplication, and omega uses the coherent rate with its exact unit interpretation. Lifted phase needs no sine, cosine or modulo operation. For a moderate magnitude whose turn quotient is representable, wrapped phase uses k=floor(phi/turn) via one multiplication and floor, followed by psi=fma(-k,turn,phi): add (0,1,1,0,0), one floor, integer conversion and boundary correction comparisons. A near-cut rounded quotient is merely the naive branch estimate. Large arguments need multiword reduction; its cost depends on exponent and cannot be hidden in a constant-cost modulo instruction. A known cycle-zero selection skips reduction.

**Calculation (Inverse time, tail and horizon)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-time`

For one supplied cycle n, delta=target-alpha, u=fma(turn,n,delta) and t_n=u*tau give (1,2,1,0,1) from L,C, or (1,1,1,0,0) with tau available. If only a is available, use u/a instead of u*tau, replacing the final M by D. For independent zero-phase rectangles with n>=0, evaluate both endpoints turn*n/a_max and turn*n/a_min; shared turn*n costs M once and the endpoints cost two D, or two M with reciprocal rates prepared. Numbering is by exact n, with ordering ambiguous for overlapping boxes, not by rounding t to an index (`rule:nrz:lc-relation-time-queries`). With w=a_max-a_min>0, the tail index ceil(a_min/w) costs A,D and ceil; the tail start turn*n/a_max adds M,D. With phase width d=alpha_max-alpha_min, H=(turn-d)/w costs (3,0,0,1,0) from the four endpoints, including forming w and d. Zero spread and whole-circle cases require explicit branches, not division by zero. These formulas have the scope of (`rule:nrz:lc-relation-overlap-tail`), (`rule:nrz:lc-relation-forward-horizon`).

**Calculation (Observation recovery and inverse elements)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-elements`

For a sign-separated pair with a retained cycle difference k, dt=t_b-t_a, dx=x_b-x_a, z=fma(turn,k,dx), tau=dt/z and P=tau*tau cost (2,1,1,1,0). To recover rate and initial phase as well, a=1/tau and alpha=fma(-a,t_a,fma(turn,n_a,x_a)) add (0,0,2,1,0). With known C, L=P/C adds D; with known L, C=P/L adds D. With supplied R=Vhat/Ihat>0, L=R*tau and C=tau/R add M,D; computing R from two amplitudes adds D. With supplied E and a positive current amplitude, L=2*E/Ihat² costs (0,2,0,1,0); voltage similarly yields C=2*E/Vhat². Recovering the other element adds D from P. Every extra observation or supplied restriction still needs its consistency comparison and original joint-fibre projection (`alg:nrz:lc-evaluation-recovery-graph`), (`alg:nrz:lc-evaluation-element-graph`). Energy without amplitude retains the scale family, so there is no finite L/C value to price as a solved singleton.

**Calculation (Energy, amplitudes and supplied state)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-energy`

E=C*Vhat²/2 or L*Ihat²/2 costs (0,3,0,0,0), reduced to two M when the fixed element times half is prepared. Both supplied amplitudes require both expressions and a consistency test. From E,L,C, h=2*E, Vhat=sqrt(h/C), Ihat=sqrt(h/L) cost (0,1,0,2,2); a single amplitude costs (0,1,0,1,1). For supplied instantaneous v,i, evaluate vv=v*v, ii=i*i, z=L*ii, E=0.5*fma(C,vv,z), costing (0,4,1,0,0). No transcendental is needed to evaluate energy from that state. Exact rest returns zero amplitudes with undefined physical phase and ratio (`rule:nrz:lc-relation-kinds`).

**Calculation (Optional forward state)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-state`

For the original orientation C*dv/dt=i in (`def:nrz:lc-resonance-model`), v=Vhat*cos(phi), i=-Ihat*sin(phi). Add two M, a sign operation and one sincos evaluation to (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-phase`). A fast naive library shares quadrant reduction between sine and cosine, evaluates two reduced-domain polynomials with vector FMA, then selects signs and quadrants. It has no hardware vector sincos instruction. If the chosen approximation uses p sine and q cosine FMAs, r other multiplications and a additions/reduction FMAs, add those explicitly plus integer reduction and exceptional-lane work; p,q,r,a depend on the chosen error target and range. A trigonometric recurrence along equally spaced times replaces repeated sincos with four M and two A per sample after preparing the rotation, but accumulates error and needs declared reseeding. No fixed throughput number is asserted for an unspecified library or arbitrary argument range; this extension must select its own algorithm before receiving an acceptance denominator.

**Calculation (Driven impedance, current and sign)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-driven`

For the separate driven plan, q=a_drive*C, r=1/q and X=fma(a_drive,L,-r) cost (0,1,1,1,0). Two sign comparisons select positive, negative or nominal zero; the phase is the selected half-pi constant, with no atan2 or trigonometric evaluation. The current's imaginary coordinate is -V/X and adds D and sign, only for a separated nonzero X. Fixed a_drive and C hoist q,r; varying L then costs one F per X. Repeated shared V may hoist its import but cannot hoist 1/X across changing reactance. Uncertain sign uses the same endpoint predicate mechanics as (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-decisions`), and exact resonance needs original-expression evidence (`rule:nrz:lc-evaluation-driven-boundary`). The full arbitrary-input X map reads three scalars and writes one, 32 bytes; adding current reads V and writes another scalar, 48 bytes total. Shared-voltage covariance and all requested statistical companion/cross blocks have their own declared output dimensions and cannot be priced as just this deterministic current.

**Calculation (Decisions and finite face families)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-decisions`

A naive scalar predicate Q<h adds one comparison to its Q inventory; comparing a two-endpoint image [lo,hi] with h adds two comparisons and selects yes, no or unresolved numerically. Region universal/existential reduction adds Boolean combination or witness-index selection; counting adds integer accumulation. These floating outcomes lack the certification of (`def:nrz:lc-compute-questions`). An independent positive rectangle computes extremal rates at (L_min,C_min) and (L_max,C_max), so it uses twice the frequency inventory; signed time uses four rate/time products, six comparisons to select both extrema and two additions of phase endpoints. Thus the rectangular lifted-phase answer costs (2,6,0,2,2) plus six comparisons before wrapping; it reads eight unrelated endpoint scalars and writes two result endpoints, 80 bytes with streaming stores. An affine face intersection x=(b_v-b_u)/(m_u-m_v) costs (2,0,0,1,0) per candidate, then evaluating fma(m,x,b) costs F per tested face. A finite topology with V candidates and H constraints costs at most V times the intersection inventory plus V*H FMAs and comparisons before reducing extrema. Correlated recovery/future questions use their actual V,H and aliases; these are not population-independent constants for unrestricted constraints (`def:nrz:lc-evaluation-fibre-recipe`), (`def:nrz:lc-evaluation-future-recipe`).

## Optimized naive batch and tile method · `sec:nrz:lc-compute-tiles`

**Calculation (Axis separation and fractional work)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-separation`

For an m by n positive L/C tile, prepare r_i=1/sqrt(L_i) and s_j=1/sqrt(C_j), then a_ij=r_i*s_j. Per instance this costs (0,1,0,h_ax,h_ax), where h_ax=1/m+1/n. Likewise tau_ij=sqrt(L_i)*sqrt(C_j) costs (0,1,0,0,h_ax). For forward phase add F per requested time, so the per-selection inventory is (0,1/J,1,h_ax/J,h_ax/J) when the rate plane is reused over J samples. If both rate and tau are requested, compare computing reciprocal tau per cell against preparing both reciprocal-root axes; choose the cheaper supported graph and count it once. This algebra changes floating rounding relative to sqrt(L*C); the uncertified reference permits that reassociation while the certified implementation must enclose the original expression. Intermediate exponent scaling is required when the factored expression would overflow despite a finite final answer. Independent physical boxes may share corresponding endpoint axes. If complete transformed axes can be prepared once and streamed across many tiles, replace h_ax in the root/divide inventory by h_ax_global=1/N_L+1/N_C for total axis lengths N_L,N_C; tile input traffic still follows the actual axis reuse and cache residency. The 64 by 64 worked example remains memory bound under that further optimization. Arbitrary independent per-cell L/C pairs have no such reuse and retain one S and one D per pair in the direct product chain.

**Calculation (Hoisting across questions)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-hoisting`

For the historical varying-L, fixed-C, fixed-t, fixed-alpha question, prepare t/sqrt(C) and evaluate phi=fma(t/sqrt(C),1/sqrt(L),alpha): per cell (0,0,1,1,1), with amortized constant setup. Generic nonsquare L is equally admissible. With a cached tau plane, inverse time uses (1,1,1,0,0) per arbitrary member; delta and turn*n can each be shared on their own axes. For consecutive n, prepare t_start and period=turn*tau, then add period per lane, so each member costs A plus preparation divided by the member extent. Use independent lane subsequences and periodic direct reseeding to bound recurrence drift. Time-axis phase similarly uses phi_start and a*delta_t, then A per member. Uniform L or C spacing does not make sqrt(L*C) linear; do not replace roots by an additive recurrence.

**Calculation (Optimized elements, energy and futures)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-projections`

With recovered P and a known element varying on an axis of length n reused m times, prepare its reciprocal once: L or C then costs (0,1,0,1/m,0) per cell. Recovery of P still costs (2,1,1,1,0) for unrelated pairs. If only the inverse element is requested and P has no other consumer, fuse the expression: L=dt²/(z²*C), or C=dt²/(z²*L), costs (2,3,1,1,0), saving a division against forming P and dividing again. This reassociation requires its own overflow/scaling treatment. A shared dt permits reciprocal-rate reconstruction by multiplication where the lifted phase denominator is also shared. From supplied tau and ratio R on an independent axis, simultaneous L/C costs two M plus reciprocal-R work amortized by its reuse count. Energy from fixed element costs two M; varying element costs three M. For both amplitudes, prepare inverse square-root element axes and evaluate sqrt(2*E) once per independent energy value, then two M per pair, with the roots and divisions divided by their actual reuse extents. A horizon with unrelated endpoint spreads retains (3,0,0,1,0); fixed w permits its reciprocal to be prepared and replaces D by M. Finite-face recovery and calibrated futures share slopes, inverse slope differences and branch coefficients only where their dependencies are fixed; the V,H work of (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-decisions`) remains visible otherwise.

**Calculation (Analytic aggregates on an ordered affine axis)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-aggregate-shortcut`

When L_j=L_start+j*delta_L with delta_L>0 is the supplied map, C>0, t>0 and h>alpha are fixed along that axis, phi<h is equivalent to L_j>L_cut, where L_cut=(t/(h-alpha))²/C. A naive row computes the cut with (1,1,0,2,0), or replaces the C division by a prepared reciprocal multiplication, then j_first=floor((L_cut-L_start)/delta_L)+1 with another A,D,floor and integer increment. Clamping the integer to the row domain gives the count and first/last qualifying index with constant work per row, rather than a scan. Fixed reciprocal spacing replaces the last D by M. Equality, t=0, negative t, h<=alpha and clipped domains require their corresponding branch formulas. Uncertain physical inputs and joint-source quantifiers require their own extrema and existence laws; the certified shortcut additionally requires the authenticated map and cover of (`def:nrz:lc-grid-map-workload`). Arbitrary host axes or unsupported predicates retain the scan model, including the worked decision/count examples below, which assume no ordered affine-map premise. A full decision map still writes its packed bits, even when range filling replaces comparisons; a full value map still evaluates every requested value. For one row the direct cut/index arithmetic is (2,1,0,3,0), one floor and integer clamp/count operations. Reading C,t,alpha,h,L_start,delta_L once costs 48 bytes; writing a count costs eight bytes, or an index/status pair sixteen. Shared row parameters reduce those reads. For a row of length n these fixed operations and bytes divide by n; the total for one analytic row therefore does not grow when n changes from 10⁶ to 10⁹. For many independent rows, apply (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-roofline`) to their row inventory, then divide by n only when reporting a represented-cell quotient. For this ordered-axis aggregate workload the reference denominator is per row, with the preparation divided by row population, and (`rule:nrz:lc-grid-ratio-information`) requires the certified comparison to use that faster reference rather than the scan estimate. The remaining shared-work attribution choices are (`open:nrz:lc-compute-attribution`). This is why output form and map premises must accompany every overhead ratio.

**Definition (Cache layout and vector occupancy)** · `def:nrz:lc-compute-layout`

Use separate contiguous scalar planes with the inner varying axis contiguous, broadcast fixed operands, and process four binary64 lanes per AVX2 vector. Unroll independent vectors to hide operation latency; tail and sparse selections lower effective occupancy. A model tile of m=n=64 has 4096 instances; a rate plane is 32 KiB and two input axes total 1024 bytes. A few value planes fit the per-core L2 in (`fact:nrz:lc-compute-machine`), while a small inner strip fits L1. Certification adds companion and mask planes, so reduce tile or strip extent when the live inventory exceeds cache; 4096 is an illustration, never an engine default. Fuse producer/consumer arithmetic where allowed and retain reusable planes only when cheaper than recomputation. Stream complete aligned output cache lines with non-temporal stores when the map will not be reused soon. Ordinary cached stores may add read-for-ownership and later writeback. Gathered inputs, compaction, reverse index maps, partial masks and live prior readers add their actual traffic under (`rule:nrz:lc-evaluation-branch-layout`).

**Calculation (Read and write bytes for every output form)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-traffic`

Let r be distinct binary64 input scalars fetched from memory per numerical selection after reuse. Arbitrary L,C,t,alpha has r=4; fixed-C forward has r=1; unrelated inverse-time L,C,alpha,target has r=4 with n generated; P recovery from two time/phase pairs has r=4, plus one known element for inverse L/C; energy from element and amplitude has r=2; both amplitudes from E,L,C has r=3; state energy has r=4; horizon has r=4 endpoint scalars. A rectangular L/C grid with fixed t and alpha has r=h_ax from axis reads per tile, or less with cross-tile axis reuse. An independently stored threshold adds one to r; a broadcast threshold adds its once-per-tile bytes divided by filled cells. Returning a native cycle number or original-index map adds its explicit integer bytes, commonly eight per selection, to q-plane traffic. A full q-plane map reads 8r and writes 8q bytes per selection. A packed naive decision map reads 8r and writes 1/8 byte for a binary predicate, or 1/4 byte for two bits expressing three numerical outcomes. A count or scalar aggregate reads 8r, writes O(8) bytes per final aggregate and keeps local accumulators in registers; a first/last result reads 8r per examined selection and writes an index/status pair, O(16) bytes per result. A vector reduction's arithmetic or integer cost remains even when its map is not stored. These are memory-interface bytes, not all L1 loads: loading a retained intermediate plane adds 8 bytes to L1 traffic even if it never leaves cache. At most one final streaming write is assumed; a downstream reread of the full map adds another 8q bytes, and an ordinary write-allocate map can cost 16q bytes instead of 8q at the memory interface.

## Machine model and binding terms · `sec:nrz:lc-compute-machine-model`

**Fact (Reference node and documented resources)** · `fact:nrz:lc-compute-machine`

The reference class is a Ryzen 9 5950X with one active thread per physical core. [AMD's processor specification](https://www.amd.com/en/products/processors/desktops/ryzen/5000-series/amd-ryzen-9-5950x.html) documents sixteen cores, a 3.4 GHz base clock, 8 MiB total L2, 64 MiB total L3 and DDR4 up to 3200 MT/s. This model fixes 3.4 GHz rather than assuming boost. [AMD publication 56665, revision 3.00](https://docs.amd.com/v/u/en-US/56665), sections 2.6 and 2.11–2.12, documents the Zen 3 core's 32 KiB L1 data cache, 512 KiB L2, 256-bit floating execution, two vector loads and one vector store per cycle, and shared divide/square-root execution. Its core-level model is applied to the desktop Zen 3 class; EPYC socket bandwidth is not transferred to this node. The two 32 MiB last-level cache domains are not a single uniform private cache. Frequency, channel population and memory traffic are explicit reference assumptions, not measurements of the historical environment (`obs:nrz:lc-grid-measurement-conditions`).

**Hypothesis (Arithmetic resource coefficients pending source rows)** · `hyp:nrz:lc-compute-throughputs`

Pending the instruction rows in the companion workbook to AMD publication 56665 revision 3.00, assume for four-lane vectors two add, two multiply or two FMA instructions per core-cycle on their eligible pipelines: eight scalar operations of each kind per cycle, with multiply, FMA and floating comparison sharing eligible resources. Let rho_div and rho_sqrt stand for the not-yet-available documented reciprocal throughputs in core-cycles per four-lane divide and square-root instruction respectively; preserve them separately, since a divide is not one generic flop. The assumed simple-operation rates must also be checked against those rows; no workbook figure is represented as already verified. The shared divider term is (rho_div*D+rho_sqrt*S)/4 core-cycles per selection. Under the assumed distinct add and multiply/FMA pipelines, mixed simple floating work costs max(A/8,(M+F+N_cmp)/8) core-cycles, where N_cmp counts floating comparisons. The pending rows must confirm this overlap and comparison eligibility; if they do not, the affected mixed schedule must be revised. Floor/conversion, mask extraction, integer reductions, dispatch and memory ports have their own terms. Vector throughput is not single-instruction latency; enough independent vectors are required. Denormal or huge-argument fallback is outside this normal-lane estimate and is charged separately. The numerical instantiation and source rows for rho_div and rho_sqrt are an explicit source prerequisite under (`open:nrz:lc-compute-machine-evidence`).

**Hypothesis (Sustainable bandwidth and load assumptions)** · `hyp:nrz:lc-compute-bandwidth`

Populate two 64-bit DDR4-3200 channels: the transfer-rate ceiling is 2*8*3.2=51.2 GB/s, not sustainable application bandwidth. For the worked reference assume B_one=12.8 GB/s and B_node=38.4 GB/s, respectively one quarter and three quarters of that ceiling, with sequential traffic, balanced core placement and no competing load. The quarter and three-quarter fractions are chosen only for illustration, not from an efficiency source. These are model parameters, not manufacturer promises or measured values. Manufacturer specifications alone do not establish sustainable memory bandwidth. For sensitivity, let each bandwidth parameter range independently over the illustrative interval [12.8,38.4] GB/s, subject to B_one<=B_node for the same traffic mix; this is not an evidenced sustainable-bandwidth interval. Its memory term ranges from b/38.4 to b/12.8 ns, a factor of three, before taking the maximum with compute. Keep B_mem symbolic outside that illustration rather than relabelling peak bandwidth as sustainable. No bandwidth benchmark is called for by this concept. This evidence limitation is retained in (`open:nrz:lc-compute-machine-evidence`) before any acceptance denominator is fixed.

**Calculation (Resource composition and population)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-roofline`

Let c=max(A/8,(M+F+N_cmp)/8,(rho_div*D+rho_sqrt*S)/4,c_integer,R_L1/64,W_L1/32) be the idealized core-cycle demand per selection, using the inventories above, aligned byte rates and sufficient independent vectors. This deliberately conservative simple-FP sharing term is a schedule assumption, while the divider demand adds root and divide occupancy because they share a resource. Let b=8r+w be memory bytes, with output w from (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-traffic`). The steady-state reference is t_p=max(c/(3.4*p),b/B_p) nanoseconds per selection, with B_p in GB/s and p active physical cores; B_p=B_one for p=1 and B_p=B_node for p=16. Compute and memory overlap in this roofline; disjoint setup, barriers or flush time do not overlap by definition and add separately. This is a throughput reference floor/estimate under its assumptions, not an upper bound on program latency. At 10⁶ selections, t nanoseconds gives t milliseconds; at 10⁹ it gives t seconds. Startup and final reductions add T_setup+T_finish, and actual time is at least the critical-path/dependency latency if the batch is too short to saturate resources. The p=16 estimate assumes independent instance tiles and no serial aggregate bottleneck.

**Calculation (Shared-axis value maps become memory limited)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-map-bound`

For m=n=64, J=B=1, fixed t and alpha, h_ax=1/32 and forward phase has M=1,F=1,D=S=h_ax. The inner strip broadcasts r_i and loads a four-lane s_j vector per four selections, about 8 L1 read bytes per selection; the 64 axis values do not all remain in registers. Ignoring integer loop overhead and amortized broadcasts, c=max(1/4,(rho_div+rho_sqrt)/128,8/64,8/32). Thus if rho_div+rho_sqrt<=32, c=1/4 cycle. A streamed scalar map has b=8+8h_ax=8.25 bytes, giving t_one=max(0.07353,0.64453)=0.64453 ns and t_node=max(0.00460,0.21484)=0.21484 ns with (`hyp:nrz:lc-compute-bandwidth`). The corresponding 10⁶ totals are 0.645 ms and 0.215 ms, and the 10⁹ totals 0.645 s and 0.215 s, before setup. A full-map reread doubles its output-plane traffic contribution. The output plane, not the roots, binds in this separated workload; the conclusion survives any rho_div+rho_sqrt up to the stated threshold. At 10⁶ cells an 8 MB map may remain in L3 if reused; these numbers deliberately describe first materialization to memory, not a warm-cache rerun. At 10⁹ its 8 GB map exceeds aggregate cache, so cache residency cannot remove the streaming term.

**Calculation (Decision and aggregate outputs change the binding resource)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-decision-bound`

On the same separated phase tile, a threshold comparison adds N_cmp=1 and a binary decision map writes 1/8 byte. With no per-cell threshold load, b=0.375 bytes and c=max(3/8,(rho_div+rho_sqrt)/128,c_integer,1/8), where c_integer is the unsourced packing/reduction and loop demand in cycles per selection. Thus t_one=max(c/3.4,0.02930) and t_node=max(c/54.4,0.00977) ns. A count without a map has b=0.25, giving t_one=max(c/3.4,0.01953) and t_node=max(c/54.4,0.00651), with its own reduction coefficient in c_integer. A first/last scan uses its examined E selections and its own index-reduction demand, plus final reduction time. These figures give the same numerical milliseconds at 10⁶ and seconds at 10⁹. At c_integer=1/2 and rho_div+rho_sqrt<=64, c=1/2; at c_integer<=3/8 and rho_div+rho_sqrt<=48, c=3/8. Neither coefficient is established by an instruction source, so neither is selected as the reference. For a prepared phase with no per-selection rate multiplication, the floating-only demand instead falls to 1/4, and its floating/integer term is max(1/4,c_integer), before load and other resource terms (`pred:nrz:lc-compute-overhead-envelope`). Loading a different threshold per cell adds 8 bytes; recompute the binding maximum with the unresolved integer coefficient.

**Calculation (Question-specific resource tests)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-question-bounds`

For fixed-C forward, c=max(1/8,(rho_div+rho_sqrt)/4,1/4), b_value=16, b_decision=8.125 and b_aggregate=8; the one-core memory terms are 1.25, 0.63477 and 0.625 ns, and the node terms are 0.41667, 0.21159 and 0.20833 ns. For unrelated L,C,t,alpha forward, simple arithmetic adds M with the same divider term, with b_value=40, b_decision=32.125 and b_aggregate=32: memory terms are 3.125, 2.50977 and 2.5 ns on one core, and 1.04167, 0.83659 and 0.83333 ns on the node. The binding term is the larger of each number and c/(3.4*p); decision and aggregate also add their comparison/reduction demand. This exposes why the same phase formula can be arithmetic limited with one varying input but memory limited with four.

**Calculation (Projection and energy resource tests)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-projection-bounds`

An arbitrary inverse-time member from L,C,alpha,target has c=max(1/8,3/8,rho_sqrt/4,32/64,8/32) and b_value=40; its memory terms are 3.125 ns on one core and 1.04167 ns on the node. With tau already shared, its separate add and multiply/FMA demands and two varying phase inputs instead give c>=1/4 and b_value=24 unless delta is also shared. The optimized inverse-element-only query from five unrelated scalars has (2,3,1,1,0), c>=max(2/8,4/8,rho_div/4,40/64) and b_value=48; memory terms are 3.75 and 1.25 ns. If P and the element are both requested, keep (2,1,1,2,0), c>=max(2/8,2/8,rho_div/2,40/64,16/32) and b_value=56, with memory terms 4.375 and 1.45833 ns. Energy from element/amplitude has c>=max(3/8,16/64,8/32)=3/8 and b_value=24, hence 1.875 and 0.625 ns, memory limited. Both amplitudes have c>=max(1/8,(rho_div+rho_sqrt)/2,24/64,16/32), b_value=40, giving memory terms 3.125 and 1.04167 ns; compare the divider term explicitly. Supplied-state energy has c>=max(5/8,32/64,8/32)=5/8 and b_value=40, hence 3.125 and 1.04167 ns, memory limited. Horizon has c>=max(3/8,rho_div/4,32/64,8/32), b_value=40, with the same memory terms. For each scalar-output row, replace its 8-byte write by 1/8 byte for a predicate map or zero for an aggregate, and add comparison/reduction cycles; for the amplitude pair replace 16 bytes and declare which predicate consumes the pair. Multiplying each resulting max by 10⁶ or 10⁹ gives milliseconds or seconds as in (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-roofline`). The driven X map has c>=max(2/8,rho_div/4,24/64,8/32) and memory terms 2.5 and 0.83333 ns; the X/current pair has c>=max(2/8,rho_div/2,32/64,16/32) and memory terms 3.75 and 1.25 ns. Tail and finite-face queries use their explicit operation multiplicities in (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-time`), (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-decisions`); a fixed V,H instance has the same scale rule, while growing aliases/faces multiply the computation and may change the binding term.

**Calculation (Binding thresholds without unavailable throughput numbers)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-crossovers`

The symbolic tests already determine the binding resource conditionally, without inventing rho_div or rho_sqrt. For fixed-C forward, value-map compute binds on one core when rho_div+rho_sqrt>17 and on the full node when rho_div+rho_sqrt>90.667; otherwise memory binds, with equality balanced. For its binary decision map the thresholds are 8.633 and 46.042, and for aggregate/first-last scan they are 8.5 and 45.333, provided the extra mask/reduction term fits. For four-input forward, the corresponding thresholds are 42.5 and 226.667 for values, 34.133 and 182.042 for decision maps, and 34 and 181.333 for aggregates. These follow by solving (rho_div+rho_sqrt)/(4*3.4*p)>b/B_p and hold at both 10⁶ and 10⁹ under the streaming assumptions.

For inverse time from four inputs the same value-map test uses rho_sqrt>42.5 on one core or rho_sqrt>226.667 on the node. For inverse element alone, value-map compute binds when rho_div>51 or rho_div>272, respectively; publishing both P and the element gives thresholds rho_div>29.75 or rho_div>158.667. For the amplitude pair the value-map thresholds are rho_div+rho_sqrt>21.25 and rho_div+rho_sqrt>113.333. Horizon's corresponding thresholds are rho_div>42.5 and rho_div>226.667. Driven X uses rho_div>34 and rho_div>181.333; X plus current uses rho_div>25.5 and rho_div>136. Energy and supplied-state energy are memory bound with the assumed simple-operation rates. For any of these questions and any of the three output forms, the general test is (rho_div*D+rho_sqrt*S)>4*3.4*p*(8r+w)/B_p after checking the simple arithmetic, integer and cache-port terms; w is 8q, 1/8 or zero, respectively. This supplies the decision/aggregate crossover as well as its time, without silently making an unavailable instruction coefficient numerical. Multiple endpoints, faces or outputs use their own r,q and inventory before this test; they are not priced by a favorable scalar surrogate.

## What certification adds · `sec:nrz:lc-compute-certification`

**Definition (Distinct certification components)** · `def:nrz:lc-compute-components`

Compare the same input supports, sample/branch selections, requested q outputs and output form. The naive point inventory is a building block; when the requested answer has several endpoints or face candidates, both sides evaluate that inventory for the same selections. Physical support width is not numerical error. The certified reading law keeps center, deterministic-total radius and numerical-only error under (`rule:nrz:lc-evaluation-companions`); numerical error is included once in total, not added twice. The following components distinguish arithmetic work, bytes and exact work so the same cost is not charged twice. Their counts are conceptual schedules to be proved by later numerical-law design, not new operation implementations.

**Calculation (Radius propagation)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-radius`

For a product center x*y with total radii r_x,r_y, the input-uncertainty contribution abs(x)*r_y+abs(y)*r_x+r_x*r_y costs three M and two A; using two FMA changes this to one M and two F without changing the bound obligation. Addition propagates r_x+r_y with one A. For fma(x,y,z), add r_z to the product contribution, another A. A separate numerical-only channel repeats the applicable error propagation on its own input errors, retaining original-source interactions where the law requires them. A square-root bound uses r_x/(sqrt(x-r_x)+sqrt(x)) on a positive interval; a prepared positive lower denominator permits a multiplication by its upper reciprocal instead, at the expense of width. Reciprocal propagation uses r_x/(abs(x)*(abs(x)-r_x)) with a separated positive denominator. Choosing direct endpoint roots/divisions or shared lower-bound coefficients changes S,D and width; the chosen recipe must be named. Carrying both companion channels adds sixteen input bytes per varying scalar when read from memory; registers and reused axes can avoid repeated memory traffic, but cannot remove propagation arithmetic.

**Calculation (Rounding enclosure)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-rounding`

For each normal rounded result z, a precomputed outward coefficient eta>=u/(1-u), u=2^-53, gives an absolute rounding contribution eta*abs(z), with a subnormal-safe additive allowance when needed. Computing this term and adding it to a radius costs M,A; rounding of those bound operations needs its own proven inflation, for example a final multiplication by a prepared outward gamma coefficient. Thus a simple a-priori schedule budgets M,A,M per rounded center operation per independently maintained bound channel, with coefficient preparation shared. An error-free product instead adds fma(x,y,-rounded_product), one F plus absolute-value and bound accumulation; TwoSum adds six A for a general sum residual. Do not charge both alternatives to the same rounding. Roots, divisions, scaling and exceptional results need their stated laws; eta alone is not a complete root/reciprocal uncertainty formula. No rounding-mode switch belongs to this schedule (`rule:nrz:lc-grid-fast-path`).

**Calculation (Cycle and decision evidence application)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-membership`

The tile prelude supplies outward turn bounds and inward stratum bounds backed by retained pi evidence. A scalar interval predicate forms center minus/plus radius with two A and compares both endpoints to the appropriate inward threshold bounds, two comparisons, followed by mask operations. A fixed cycle membership test compares both endpoints with two owned faces, up to four comparisons plus endpoint formation; wrapping a selected branch adds an enclosed turn multiple and subtraction for each requested output. Integer n import and its error are shared by branch identity, never used to infer that identity. Combining a cycle test with a threshold test reuses endpoints and pays only the additional comparisons. A failed separation goes to its charged barrier/refinement outcome; a native comparison cannot establish an exact equality. Pi construction and full proof rechecking are not per-cell costs (`rule:nrz:lc-grid-pi-reuse`).

**Calculation (Masks and output companions)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-certified-bytes`

Each independently stored mask consumes ceil(K_tile/64)*8 bytes; its asymptotic one-bit cost is 1/8 byte per selection. A read plus write of one mask costs 1/4 byte, with exact padding charged on partial tiles. Filled, yes, no, undecided and refusal are distinct roles; one bit is not a three-state outcome. Shared immutable masks can serve compatible roles without copying (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-coverage`). Center plus one radius writes 16q bytes versus naive 8q; the required separate numerical-only channel raises a full three-plane reading to 24q bytes. This is an additional sixteen bytes per scalar output, not a second arithmetic radius charge. For a decision-only or aggregate output, transient centers/radii can remain in cache and be retired; retaining them for later consumers adds their actual writes. With r varying inputs and q outputs and no reuse, three-plane value traffic is 24r+24q plus mask traffic, compared with 8r+8q. It approaches a factor of three in a memory-bound case, not automatically four or eight. For the phase/threshold/cycle example in (`pred:nrz:lc-compute-overhead-envelope`), count four separately written result masks: yes, no, undecided and cycle membership. Read the three input filled masks for a,t,alpha and intersect them with two wordwise AND operations per full 64-selection word, after checked cell correspondence. This assumes each input's companions share its coverage; independent companion masks add their own reads and intersections. Four writes plus three reads cost 7/8 byte per selection before padding. An output filled role may share the retained intersection; storing a new intersection adds another mask write. Refusal or other roles add their actual traffic and integer work unless they share a compatible immutable allocation. Broadcast inputs instead read and validate their singleton coverage once per reuse scope; their checked expansions and any cached intersections are charged to that scope (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-demand`). Define c_integer_cert=4*c_pack+c_intersect+c_select+c_loadmask+c_other, in cycles per selection for a schedule that adds demands sharing integer resources. Here c_pack covers extraction, shift/or packing and padding for one result role; c_intersect covers the input-coverage intersection; c_select covers filled-set restriction and outcome/cycle Boolean combination; c_loadmask covers set-bit/word traversal and safe masked loads or bounded packing; c_other includes loop/index and any extra roles. Account for load/store port demand separately, without duplicating its time as integer work. Dense full strips still require validated coverage, while partial strips cannot speculatively load holes. No coefficient is sourced here: even four illustrative c_pack=1/2 contributions give 2 cycles and leave only 1/8 cycle below 17/8 for all remaining integer work. The certified integer term must therefore remain in the maximum until its schedule is established.

**Calculation (Exact work and exceptional fractions)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-exact-amortization`

For a tile with k>0 filled cells, let W_tile be exact preparation, new attachments and charged lookup Work not reused elsewhere, and T_tile its separately predicted time from a specified exact-operation schedule. Their per-cell contributions are W_tile/k and T_tile/k; Work cannot be converted to time by calling it a flop. Shared setup is divided by its actual reuse population; a decision barrier can span several tiles or less than one (`rule:nrz:lc-stream-tile-size`). If fraction f needs a fallback of time T_f per affected cell, add f*T_f, preserving failed attempts and prior results. For no filled cells, there is no per-cell quotient. With distinct uncached semantic content W_tile can grow with k; no bound follows merely from tiling or physical distance from a threshold (`def:nrz:lc-grid-map-workload`). Per-tile checks of target versions and mask capacity/layout before value access retain a separately charged validation component under (`rule:nrz:tiles-streams-mask-coverage`); they are not full proof revalidation. Index/association construction, source restoration, compaction, publication and flush each retain a separate charged component if present.

**Calculation (An explicit forward-phase companion schedule)** · `calc:nrz:lc-compute-phase-companions`

For a prepared a and an already available t,alpha selection, the center phi=fma(a,t,alpha) costs F. A provisional two-channel radius schedule uses one M, two F and one A per channel for the product-radius contribution and the added alpha radius, then two M and one A per channel for the rounding term and final proven inflation. Thus the complete phase arithmetic is (4,6,5,0,0), before membership: center F, propagation (2,2,4,0,0), and rounding (2,4,0,0,0). Forming endpoints and one threshold verdict adds two A and two comparisons. The simple-pipeline demand is then max(6/8,13/8)=13/8 cycles, versus 1/8 for the center alone, or the appropriate comparison/packing denominator for a naive decision. This does not assert a thirteenfold wall-time cost: when cached inputs feed a streamed scalar value map, the memory write changes from 8 to 24 bytes, giving a threefold traffic term, and that term can hide the extra arithmetic. Input traffic, other mask roles and preparation remain as separate components. The later law must prove the inflation for all bound operations and any original-source interactions; if it requires extra terms, their counts replace this provisional schedule. This example makes clear why resource composition and both companion channels must be resolved before assigning a single overhead factor.

**Prediction (Conditional local factor and the four-to-eightfold budget test)** · `pred:nrz:lc-compute-overhead-envelope`

A prediction is made by summing resource demands before taking their maximum: t_cert=max(c_cert/(3.4*p),b_cert/B_p)+T_tile/k+f*T_f, for disjoint barrier time, and ratio t_cert/t_naive under identical scope. Consider a prepared phase-and-threshold subgraph with resident input strips and a declared cycle face. Its naive center plus predicate costs F and one comparison, so its floating/integer term is max(1/4,c_integer), with the unsourced naive coefficient of (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-decision-bound`). The certified schedule in (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-phase-companions`) costs (6,6,5,0,0) plus two threshold comparisons and four cycle-face comparisons; its simple floating demand is max(6/8,17/8)=17/8 cycles. Its integer demand is c_integer_cert from (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-certified-bytes`), including four result-mask roles, input filled-mask intersection and safe load handling. Thus the floating/integer ratio is max(17/8,c_integer_cert)/max(1/4,c_integer). Only if c_integer_cert<=17/8 does it reduce to (17/8)/max(1/4,c_integer): 4.25 at c_integer=1/2, rising to 8.5 at c_integer<=1/4. This is the conditional range for 0<=c_integer<=1/2, not a sourced choice of denominator or a bound on certified integer work. If c_integer_cert>17/8, it binds and raises the numerator.

Loads, stores and memory traffic must also fit for that floating/integer ratio to describe kernel time. Three varying scalar inputs require 24 bytes of naive L1 loads or 72 bytes for all three certified planes, giving 3/8 or 9/8 load cycles. For this case the full core demands are at least max(1/4,c_integer,3/8) and max(17/8,c_integer_cert,9/8), before mask loads/stores and other port demand; the 8.5 endpoint is not its full kernel ratio. With only a varying and t,alpha broadcast, the value-load terms fall to 1/8 and 3/8 plus amortized broadcasts, making the floating/integer range relevant if all remaining demands fit. Packed mask traffic and integer work must both fit, including the coverage and load-handling schedule of (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-certified-bytes`); otherwise use the enlarged resource maximum. Memory-output terms remain separate for each requested output form. These conditions must be checked, not presumed from cache residency.

Let R be the resulting kernel-time ratio after those maxima, and e=T_tile/(k*t_naive) the disjoint exact/barrier contribution, with fallback adding f*T_f/t_naive. With no fallback, an eightfold local budget requires 0<=e<=8-R and is impossible when R>8. Under the conditional floating/integer case with c_integer=1/2 and c_integer_cert<=17/8 and all other demands fitting, R=4.25 and e<=3.75; at c_integer<=1/4 the same conditional case has R=8.5 before any exact work. No exact allowance can bring that case down to eight. Retain the general allowance T_tile<=k*t_naive*(8-R) only when its right-hand side is nonnegative. The full question adds the frequency producer, input preparation and all other phases to both sides; no local factor is an end-to-end result. The illustrative amortization population of 4096 can span several resident strips and is not a tile-size default.

A second regime explains why that factor is not universal. A full value map changes from one plane per scalar input/output to three, tripling its dominant memory traffic. Provided the separately inventoried radius, rounding, membership and integer work and mask traffic fit the resulting memory time, its kernel subtotal is about three times the corresponding reference. Smaller radii with prepared bounds may reduce counts; thin/partial tiles, cache misses, many aliases and boundary fallbacks may increase them. The four-to-eightfold expectation in (`claim:nrz:lc-closing-overhead-budget`) remains a workload budget to establish, not a range derived here or a universal bound. The later design must discharge the integer coefficients, exact-operation allowance and resource-overlap assumptions for each complete workload, including the analytic aggregate shortcut (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-aggregate-shortcut`). The about-tenfold closing requirement remains unchanged and unproved (`claim:nrz:lc-closing-overhead-bound`).

**Criterion (Avoidable costs do not enter the reference budget)** · `crit:nrz:lc-compute-avoidable`

Per-cell pi reconstruction or full tile-proof revalidation, scalar descriptor reconstruction inside a native pass, rounding-mode switching, unconditional scalarization, repeated invariant roots, unnecessary map materialization and losing vectorization on dense full tiles are avoidable costs, not irreducible certification requirements. Authentication of actual source versions and membership still occurs at its correct scope; removing repeated proof validation never removes that obligation. A sparse or exceptional lane can legitimately reduce occupancy, but the report names its population and cost. A reference made artificially slow by omitted axis reuse or an unvectorized transcendental call cannot justify the overhead bound (`rule:nrz:lc-grid-ratio-information`).

## Open questions for the overhead-tracking design · `sec:nrz:lc-compute-open`

**Open Question (Machine evidence and sustainable bandwidth)** · `open:nrz:lc-compute-machine-evidence`

Which exact manufacturer instruction rows and operand classes fix rho_div and rho_sqrt, and which documented controller/traffic model justifies sustainable B_mem for the selected reference node? Which instruction schedules and resource eligibility establish the naive c_integer and certified c_integer_cert, including each mask packing role, input filled-mask intersection, Boolean selection and masked-load or bounded-packing handling in (`calc:nrz:lc-compute-certified-bytes`)? These are prerequisites for choosing the reference denominator and the certified maximum, not free allowances. The worked algebra keeps missing coefficients explicit; do not fill them with unrelated measured latency or advertised bandwidth. Manufacturer specifications do not establish sustainable application bandwidth. The later design must either justify its declared efficiency assumptions and sensitivity interval or retain a conditional reference, without a fabricated measurement. It must distinguish cold first materialization, cache-resident reruns, normal/exceptional arithmetic and the actual core placement/frequency used by acceptance.

**Open Question (Composition and exact-operation schedule)** · `open:nrz:lc-compute-composition`

Which certification operations share execution resources, which overlap memory, and which barrier phases must add in time? Supply an exact-operation/time model for the tile allowance in (`pred:nrz:lc-compute-overhead-envelope`) and prove each selected numerical enclosure schedule, including both companion channels. Resolve divider throughput, FP comparisons, dispatch, dependency depth and mask extraction before claiming a tighter bound. An additive list of component ratios double-counts overlapped work; a single roofline maximum across sequential barriers hides work. This concept's conditional envelope is the input to that design, not its formal composition rule.

**Open Question (Shared-work attribution)** · `open:nrz:lc-compute-attribution`

How are axis roots, pi imports, repeated branch coefficients, exact cache construction and multi-tile barriers apportioned across their actual consumers without charging both parent and child? Fix the denominator for N, K, occupied cells and examined E; record cold and eligible warm reuse, partial/empty tiles, rejected attempts and eviction. Decide how recurrence reseeding and exponent scaling change fractional work, and how alternative factorized/direct formulas are selected without changing assurance or making the naive denominator slower.

**Open Question (Observability and acceptance attachment)** · `open:nrz:lc-compute-observability`

Which counters report A,M,F,D,S, comparisons, vector occupancy, floor/reduction fallbacks, bytes by memory level, each mask/companion plane and exact construction/cache/replay work? Map those to existing tracing and ledger coverage (`sec:nrz:tiles-streams-observability`), (`req:nrz:lc-evaluation-accounting`), with exclusive sampled times, independent counters and a reconciled unexplained remainder. Fix numerical ratio tolerance, interval rule and remainder limit before measurement under (`req:nrz:lc-overhead-acceptance-protocol`); they are not chosen by this concept. Attach every required question, output form, input-family and sample/alias extent to a reproducible model instance, including finite-face and calibrated-future cases. This prepares (`claim:nrz:lc-closing-overhead-tracking`); only the actual scale run and its independently reviewed evidence can close the model's measured application.
